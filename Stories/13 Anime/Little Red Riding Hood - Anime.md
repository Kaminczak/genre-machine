# Little Red Riding Hood - Anime

**Logline:** A pink-haired girl in a red cape sprints through cherry-blossom woods, late to grandmother's. Wolf-kun waits, but she has unlocked a hidden technique.

## Script

LATE LATE LATE — Red sprinted the cherry-blossom road with a cake box balanced on one hand, hood streaming, because grandmother's dojo held visiting day once a year and she had overslept AGAIN. At the torii gate stood Wolf-kun, transfer student, undefeated, tragic backstory visible from orbit. Your grandmother's dojo falls today, he announced, wind technique already spiraling the petals. I trained three years for this. Red set the cake box down with terrible care. Everyone in the prefecture knew you did not touch her when the box was down. The petals stopped mid-air. My grandmother, Red said, taught me one move. She only teaches it to people who carry cake through wind. The technique has no name; witnesses describe it as the world politely stepping aside. Wolf-kun woke on the dojo porch with a slice of cake beside him and grandmother pouring tea. Lesson one, she said. The path is not between rivals. It is between houses. Eat.
