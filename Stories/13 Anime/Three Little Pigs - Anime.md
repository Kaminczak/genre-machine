# Three Little Pigs - Anime

**Logline:** Three pig friends defend their dorms with martial arts and sweat. Wolf-senpai has a tragic backstory and a wind technique.

## Script

Three friends. One dorm complex. Zero chance of a quiet semester. Kohta trained his dorm defense in straw-mat technique, all speed and no foundation. Ren drilled the wooden stance until his palms split, then drilled it again. Sora, who never talked about her father, spent three years perfecting the brick form and let everyone call her boring. Then Wolf-senpai transferred in, undefeated, carrying a wind technique and a backstory nobody had the courage to ask about. He shattered Kohta's straw guard in a single breath. He broke Ren's wooden stance in two, and something in his eyes looked almost sorry about it. But against Sora's brick form he exhaled everything he had, and everything he had was not enough, and he finally, finally started crying. She let him inside. She put water on the stove. That is the whole technique, she said. That is all it ever was.
