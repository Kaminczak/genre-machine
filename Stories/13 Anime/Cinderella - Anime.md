# Cinderella - Anime

**Logline:** A bullied high schooler is transformed by a magical-talking-pumpkin senpai. A glass loafer left on the festival stage; the prince is the student council president.

## Script

("The Culture Festival was in ONE WEEK and Shinderera-chan was going to miss it AGAIN because her stepsisters had signed her up for every committee simultaneously, which should be impossible, the vice-president said so, but here we are. She scrubbed the gym floor alone the night before while everyone else did lantern tests, and that is when the home-ec room's ancient sewing machine STARTED TALKING. It claimed to be the club's founding advisor, sealed into the machine by overwork in 1987, and honestly nobody questioned this because club advisors absolutely have that energy. IT SEWED ALL NIGHT. The dress was the machine's masterpiece, forty years of deferred projects in one garment, and the slippers were borrowed from the science wing — tempered glass, the teacher said, TECHNICALLY safety equipment. The festival prince — student council president, glasses, tragically responsible — had spent three years signing the forms Shinderera's committees generated and had never once seen her face. He danced with her at the bonfire while her stepsisters filed a complaint about unregistered dancing. At midnight the machine's thread ran out — dramatic unraveling, VERY dramatic, petals everywhere — and she fled, losing one safety-equipment slipper on the gym steps. He checked it against three years of committee paperwork like the bureaucrat of destiny he is: the handwriting on ten thousand forms, cross-referenced. He knew her by her signature before the shoe fit. The machine attended the wedding. It has opinions about the dress. It made the dress. Both dresses. It is very tired. It has never been happier.",)
