# Cinderella - Cyberpunk

**Logline:** A back-alley body-mod tech transforms a wage-slave for one corporate gala. The chrome-glass slipper jacks out at 00:00.

## Script

("Cindy scrubbed decks — data-decks, wage-slave tier, for the Trema syndicate family that had absorbed her father's debts and then her father. The Corporate Gala came annually: the tower's one night of unlocked floors, where princes of the org chart chose their successors. Her invite was revoked by her step-supervisor out of policy, which is cruelty with a letterhead. The godmother found her crying in the server stacks. Old woman, no employee ID, no reflection in the chrome — a rogue admin, the stacks whispered, from before the merger, still holding root on systems nobody living understood. One night, she said, fingers in Cindy's datajack. I can make the tower believe you. Gown rendered at the fabric printers, face lifted past every scanner, gait-signature forged, and glass slippers — actual glass, absurd, unhackable, no chip in them at all, which is why no scanner questioned them. The prince of the tower danced with the one guest whose profile read as nothing. At 00:00 the rogue admin's root expired and every door in the building woke up hungry. Cindy ran the fire stairs barefoot-and-one-shoe as her face flickered back on. What the prince kept was the slipper: the single object in the tower with no data in it at all. He searched the org chart for a year, scanning nothing, asking feet. The tower still does not believe her. He never asked it to.",)
