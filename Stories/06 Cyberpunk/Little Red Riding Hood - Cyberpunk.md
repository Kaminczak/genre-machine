# Little Red Riding Hood - Cyberpunk

**Logline:** A delivery courier in a red hoodie weaves neon alleys with a memory-cake for grandmother's apartment. A wolf-class assassin is already in the unit.

## Script

The delivery ticket said one memory-cake, handle with care, grandmother's unit, Stack 47. Red pulled her hood up against the neon drizzle and rode the freight elevator with the basket cuffed to her wrist. The door of 47-J was open. Grandmother never left it open; her locks had locks. Inside, the vitals monitor purred a rhythm that was not grandmother's, and the figure in the sleep-pod wore her cardigan over combat chrome. What big optics you have, Red said, easing the basket down. Enhanced for low light, said the wolf-unit, sitting up. What big servos. All the better to reach you. It reached. Red let it. The basket's false bottom had already handshaked with the unit's firmware — the cake was ICE, black and patient, and it ate the assassin from the inside out in four seconds flat. Grandmother pinged from the safehouse across the Stack: good girl. Bring the real cake Sunday.
