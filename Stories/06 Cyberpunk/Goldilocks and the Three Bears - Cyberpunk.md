# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Cyberpunk

**Logline:** A blonde data-thief jacks the Ursine family server. Three firewalls, three thrones, three neural sleep-pods. The Ursine clan boots up, fast.

## Script

("The Ursine family server sat behind three firewalls, which the blonde read as an invitation. Tier one ran too hot — honeypot heat, obvious, ugly, meant to scare off script kiddies. She slid past. Tier two was too cold, legacy code no one had touched in years, brittle as old plastic. She snapped it without slowing. Tier three was just right, elegant and current, and she settled in and drank the family's data like porridge, credentials and ledgers and the private keys to everything. She should have logged off. Instead — and every runner knows this moment — she got comfortable. Found the household's sleep-pods on the internal net, three of them humming, and parked her consciousness in the smallest for just a second, just to feel what money sleeps like. The Ursines booted home at 3 A.M., three signatures lighting the intrusion board. Someone has been in my firewall, rumbled the patriarch process. Someone has been drinking my data, said the second. The smallest pod simply initiated trace. She jacked out with her ears ringing and her name half-burned, and to this day the Ursine server keeps a fourth pod open. Bait, probably. Probably.",)
