# Three Little Pigs - Cyberpunk

**Logline:** Three pig hackers raise weak, average, and military-grade ICE. A wolf-class netrunner cracks their firewalls in sequence.

## Script

Three pig runners, three tiers of ICE, one very bad night in the Trough. Nix stacked freeware barriers on a secondhand deck and called it a home node. Bram bought mid-market ice with a legit license and slept fine. Sable ran military-grade, black-market, layered nine deep, because Sable had read the incident logs. A netrunner tagged WOLF hit them in sequence. Nix's freeware peeled in four seconds and she dumped herself into Bram's node with the scream still buffering. Bram's license expired mid-assault. Both of them landed inside Sable's ninth layer while WOLF hammered the perimeter until his own rig started cooking. So he tried the maintenance channel, the old one, the chimney of every system ever built. Sable had left it open on purpose, and hot, and waiting. The Trough learned a saying that night: cheap ice is just a slower door.
