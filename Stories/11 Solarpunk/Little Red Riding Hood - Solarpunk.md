# Little Red Riding Hood - Solarpunk

**Logline:** A child in a red harvest cloak bicycles bread along a green-tunnel path. A reformed wolf accepts an invitation to tea.

## Script

The bread was still warm when Red set off down the green tunnel, the bike path the village had grown from living willow over ten patient years. Solar moths recharged on her shoulders at the sunny bends. Grandmother's cottage sat in the orchard terraces, roof thick with sedum and one proud sunflower. A wolf waited at the gate. The old kind, gray at the muzzle, the last of the hill pack that had troubled the valley in the hungry years before the replanting. He had walked a long way, and he stood very still. I have come, he said, to ask about the tea. Everyone asks eventually. Grandmother met them on the porch with three cups already out, because she kept count of the hills and knew who was left in them. They talked until the light went amber: about hunger, about the years nobody replanted, about how a valley decides to feed everything in it or nothing. Red cut the bread. The wolf, it turned out, took honey.
