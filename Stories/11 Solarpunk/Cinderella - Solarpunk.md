# Cinderella - Solarpunk

**Logline:** A communal-farm orphan is gifted hand-loomed finery by an elder. A bio-glass slipper is left in the great-hall during the consensus festival at moonset.

## Script

("The commune's Longest Night festival needed everyone's hands for a week ahead, and Ash gave hers — she was the orchard fosterling, raised by the whole village since the flood year, which mostly meant raised by whoever was busiest. Her foster-aunts meant well and worked her hard, and when festival night came they left her minding the cider press, promising to take turns relieving her that never quite came around. It was the eldest of the grandmothers who found her there — the one who kept the seed library and everyone's measurements. Child, she said, the village owes you a night, and I am the village as much as anyone. The gown came from the commons wardrobe, silk from the old world, mended by forty hands over forty years and better for every mend. The carriage was the orchard's pumpkin cart, washed and hung with solar lanterns. The slippers were the grandmother's own: glass, from before the flood, kept not as treasure but as proof that fragile things survive if enough people mind them. Midnight is when the cider press wants tending again, she said. That is all midnight is. The potter's son danced with her till then and walked her back to the press after, and stayed, and by the spring festival the whole village had — without any vote being taken, the way the real decisions happen — built the two of them a house at the orchard's edge. The slippers went back to the seed library, next to the beans. Fragile things, minded. That is the whole religion here.",)
