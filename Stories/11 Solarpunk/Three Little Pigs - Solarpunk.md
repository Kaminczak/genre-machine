# Three Little Pigs - Solarpunk

**Logline:** Three pig artisans weave homes of thatch, mycelium, and living masonry. A reformed wolf arrives to share tea.

## Script

On the terraced slope above Greenhollow, three pig artisans built with what the hill offered. Juniper wove thatch and reed, light enough to lift by hand and mend in an afternoon. Ash grew a mycelium shell, coaxed over a willow frame across a patient season. Rowan laid living masonry, stone set with lime that drank rain and stitched its own cracks shut. When the wolf came down the ridge road, the village did not bar the doors. He had walked a long way to say something difficult. Juniper's thatch let the wind through and stayed. Ash's shell breathed and stayed. Rowan's stone had been growing quietly for years and had no opinion about wolves at all. So they set out four cups and let him talk until the sun went down behind the composting sheds. Not everything that huffs at your door has come to take the roof off it.
