# Three Little Pigs - Historical Fiction

**Logline:** In an occupied village during WWII, three pig siblings run a straw printing press, a stick radio station, and a brick safe house. Oberst Wolff is closing in.

## Script

Amidst the turmoil of World War II, in a small occupied village, three pig siblings were known for their defiance against the oppressors. The youngest, Luc, operated an underground strawprinting press, distributing messages of hope and resistance. Henri, the middle sibling, used his stick-built radio station to intercept enemy transmissions and relay crucial information to the Allies. Elise, the eldest, transformed her sturdy brick home into a safe haven for refugees and spies. As the enemy's grip tightened, a cunning wolf officer named Oberst Wolff sought to dismantle their operations. He raided Luc's press and Henri's station, forcing them to seek refuge in Elise's sanctuary. Together, they devised a plan to outsmart Oberst Wolff, using their wits and resources. Their courage became a beacon of hope, proving that unity and determination could overcome the darkest of times.
