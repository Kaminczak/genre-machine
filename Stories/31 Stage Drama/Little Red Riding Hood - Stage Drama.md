# Little Red Riding Hood - Stage Drama

**Logline:** Stage right, a path of painted trees. A girl in red enters with a basket. The wolf is already in the bed, and the audience knows it.

## Script

ACT ONE. A path of painted birches crosses the stage on a slow diagonal. Upstage, half-lit, GRANDMOTHER'S COTTAGE in cutaway: bed, wardrobe, hearth. The WOLF is already in the bed. The audience can see him. The audience could always see him; that is the play. Enter RED, stage left, with basket, singing the walking-song her mother sang. She stops to gather paper flowers, and each one she picks lifts a light somewhere in the cottage, though she cannot know that. RED, to the audience: The woods are kind today. THE AUDIENCE, who can see the bed: — . She crosses. She knocks. The knock is the sound cue for the hearth to dim. WOLF, in grandmother's voice, sweetly: Come in, child. The latch is up. RED's hand on the painted door. She turns once to the house — the whole house, all of us — as if she heard a warning start and stop in nine hundred throats. RED: Grandmother sounds strange tonight. Beat. She lifts the latch. CURTAIN on Act One, and the wolf's eyes in the dark, and nobody in their seat breathing.
