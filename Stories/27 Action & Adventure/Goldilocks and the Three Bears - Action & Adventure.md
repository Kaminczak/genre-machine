# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Action & Adventure

**Logline:** A blonde explorer follows a trail into bear country hunting a lost expedition. Three camps, three caches, three sets of tracks — and something following hers.

## Script

("The expedition had been missing nineteen days when the tracker took the contract: three camps to check, high country, bear sign everywhere, bring back proof of life or proof of otherwise. Camp one sat in a burn scar, fire pit still warm — too warm, she thought, kneeling; someone fed this within the day. Camp two hung in a ravine's cold shadow, untouched for a week, tents frost-stiff. Camp three was just right and just wrong: fresh boot prints, food cached properly, bedding aired — and all of it done in the expedition's gear but none of it in the expedition's style. Someone had been living in their camps. Someone was surviving well. She bedded down in camp three to wait, rifle across her knees, and woke at dawn to find the rifle moved, propped politely out of reach, and a figure crouched at the fire making breakfast for two: a girl, feral-thin, gold hair matted, one of the expedition's lost — the mapmaker's daughter, seventeen, walked out of the wreck of camp four that nobody knew existed, and living since off the margins of her own party's supply line. They came down the mountain together, the girl leading. She knew every camp, every cache, every bear trail. Just right, the tracker said in her report, is what survival looks like when somebody's daughter does it.",)
