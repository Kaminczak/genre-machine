# Three Little Pigs - Action & Adventure

**Logline:** Three treasure-hunting pigs chase a map through a sandstone temple, a jungle ruin, and a fortress carved into a mountain. A wolf rival is one step behind them the whole way.

## Script

In a world driven by adventure, three daring pigs set out to find ancient treasures. The first pig, eager and reckless, followed a map to a desert temple made of sandstone. The second, relying on rumors, ventured into a jungle temple made of intertwined vines. The third pig, meticulous and prepared, decoded clues leading to a fortress carved into the mountainside. However, a cunning wolf, an infamous treasure hunter, was on their trail. He ransacked the sandstone temple, forcing the first pig to flee to the jungle temple. The wolf, relentless, ambushed them there, driving both pigs to the mountain fortress. The fortress, riddled with traps, proved too challenging for the wolf. A boulder trap was triggered by the third pig, chasing the wolf away. United, the pigs discovered the legendary treasure within, proving the value of wit and bravery.
