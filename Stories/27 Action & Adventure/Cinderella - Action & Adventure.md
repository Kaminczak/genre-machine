# Cinderella - Action & Adventure

**Logline:** A servant girl escapes a locked estate, crosses three provinces, and crashes the royal ball. She loses a slipper on the palace wall on the way back out.

## Script

("The estate had walls, and the walls had a rhythm, and Ella had spent three years learning it the way prisoners learn anything: completely. Her step-guardian collected her father's maps when he died — the great expedition atlases, worth a governor's ransom — and collected Ella with them, staff by paperwork, captive by economics. The Royal Ball was the one night the estate emptied. It was also, not coincidentally, the one night the palace archive — where her father's master atlas hung as a trophy of the crown's patronage — opened its doors to guests. She went over the west wall at dusk in a gown lifted from her stepsister's castoffs, hemmed for running, and boots her father's old quartermaster had built her: glass-composite toes, climbing heels, his retirement masterpiece. You will want a hard sole for palace gutters, he said, and did not ask why. The ball was cover; the atlas was the object; the prince was the complication. He caught her not stealing it — studying it, tracing a coastline with one finger, crying. Her father's hand. Her father's coasts. The last map he drew before the sea took him. They talked cartography for two hours while the ball glittered below, which is, if you are two particular people, the most romantic possible use of a ball. At midnight the estate's staff would start home; the wall's rhythm resumed; she ran. The gutter took one boot, glass toe wedged in the palace downspout like a climber's flag on a summit. He extracted it with an archivist's care, read the maker's mark, found the quartermaster in a week, and the quartermaster — retirement masterpiece, he repeated, arms folded — would say only: the girl it fits can climb your palace. Try that instead of the fittings. The prince, to his eternal credit, opened the archive and posted an invitation: the atlas returned to the family of the man who drew it, if that family can come collect it. She came over the east wall at noon, in front of everyone, one boot on. The atlas hangs in their map room now. The wall's rhythm is whatever she says it is.",)
