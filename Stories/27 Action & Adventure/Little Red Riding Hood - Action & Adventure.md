# Little Red Riding Hood - Action & Adventure

**Logline:** A girl in a red climbing hood cuts through canyon country to grandmother's outpost, chasing a map her mother left. A wolf-guide offers to show her the shortcut.

## Script

The map was her mother's last gift, and it ended at grandmother's outpost in the canyon country — three days of switchbacks, rope bridges, and one aggressively personable guide who attached himself at the trailhead. Wolfe, he said, tipping his hat. I know a shortcut. The shortcut was real, which surprised her; they always are, that is the trap. It saved half a day and delivered them to the gorge bridge at dusk, where Wolfe's smile got one tooth too wide and his machete came out, and he explained, apologetically, that the map was worth more than the girl carrying it. The map, Red agreed, stepping backward onto the bridge, is worth a great deal. She had read it, though — all of it, including the margin note in her mother's hand about the bridge's fourth plank. Wolfe, pursuing, had not. The gorge is deep, and the river at the bottom forgiving, and by the time he finished the swim to the far bank, Red was up the last switchback, where grandmother waited with the lamps lit and the second copy of the map, and dinner.
