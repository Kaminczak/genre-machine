# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Biography Memoir

**Logline:** Decades later, Goldie writes the memoir nobody asked for: the break-in, the porridge, the trial, and what the Ursine family were actually like.

## Script

("Everyone knows the story, and everyone has it wrong, and since I am eighty-three and the last living principal, we will do this once, properly. Yes, I went in. The door of that house had been left not just unlocked but open, and I was nine, and I had been walking since the county home turned me out at dawn with a bread heel and a God-bless. The porridge: I ate the small bowl because the big one would have fed a man and I was afraid of the man it would feed. The chair broke because it was already cracked — I have said this for seventy years and I will say it in the ground — it was cracked, and I was a wisp of a thing, and it went, and I cried over it, which no version of the story keeps. The bed I make no excuse for. I was nine and it was soft. As for the Ursines: what the story calls a fright was Mrs. Ursine catching me at the window, and what nobody prints is that she caught me by the collar and fed me a proper breakfast before she said one word about the chair. I came back Saturdays to work off the damage. I came back long after it was worked off. They stood up at my wedding, the three of them, on the groom's side, because I had no side of my own. The story made them monsters I ran from. They were the first door that ever stood open on purpose.",)
