# Three Little Pigs - Biography Memoir

**Logline:** Sir Oinkster, Swineston's most revered architect, writes his memoirs — straw cottage to wooden cabin to brick mansion — with a critic named Mr. Lupus sharpening him at every step.

## Script

In the bustling town of Swineston, Sir Oinkster, a wise old pig, penned his life's memoirs. As a young piglet, Sir Oinkster had lived in a modest straw cottage. As he grew, he moved to a more resilient wooden cabin, where he learned the trades of carpentry and masonry. With time and dedication, he built a grand brick mansion, becoming Swineston's most revered architect. His life wasn't without challenges. Mr. Lupus, a notorious critic, constantly challenged his architectural designs. But with each critique, Sir Oinkster only improved, mastering his craft. His memoir, "From Straw to Stone," became a beacon of inspiration, teaching generations about perseverance and growth.
