# Three Little Pigs - Western

**Logline:** Three pig brothers stake frontier claims — tent, shack, stone homestead. A black-hatted wolf rides in at sundown.

## Script

Three Pig brothers came west and staked three claims out past the Bitterfork. Youngest, Early, threw up a canvas tent and called it done by suppertime. Middle boy, Hollis, nailed together a shack out of green pine that whistled when the wind got ideas. Eldest, Amos, spent a whole summer hauling river stone and got laughed at for every load. Come sundown one October a wolf rode in wearing a black hat and no particular hurry. He put a shoulder through Early's canvas like it was a rumor. Kicked Hollis's pine wall till the nails let go one by one. Then he got to Amos's stone, and he pushed, and the stone did what stone does, which is nothing at all. So he climbed for the chimney, which is a young man's mistake in an old man's hat. Amos had beans on. Been on all day.
