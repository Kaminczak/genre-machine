# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Western

**Logline:** A blonde drifter slips into a cabin on the bear-country trail. Three plates of beans, three rocking chairs, three bunks. The Bear gang reins up.

## Script

("She came off the bear-country trail at dusk, yellow hair under a drover's hat, horse lamed two miles back. The cabin sat unlocked, the way cabins are in country where locks insult your neighbors, and inside were three plates of beans still steaming, three rocking chairs on the porch boards, three bunks against the wall. She ate the small plate — the big one scalding, the middle one gone cold — busted the small rocker testing it, which shamed her, and fell asleep in the small bunk with her boots on, which shamed her worse. The Bear gang rode up at moonrise: Big Jim Bear, his missus Martha, and the boy they called Cub. Three horses, three tempers, one busted rocker. Someone ate my beans, said Big Jim, mild as distant thunder. Someone busted my chair, said Martha, less mild. Cub just pointed at his bunk. She woke looking down three brims. What happened next depends on who is telling it, but the facts run so: she split wood for a week to square the chair, Martha taught her beans, and when she rode out it was on a horse the Bears shod themselves. Bear country forgives most things. It never forgets a busted rocker.",)
