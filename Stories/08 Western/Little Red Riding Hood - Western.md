# Little Red Riding Hood - Western

**Logline:** A young woman in a red shawl rides to grandmother's homestead. A wolfish drifter in a dead man's hat sips coffee on the porch.

## Script

She rode out at first light with a red shawl her grandmother had knitted and a basket of biscuits her mother had sworn over. Two days to the homestead if the passes held. On the trail a drifter fell in beside her, all courtesy and dust, hat brim low, and asked after her road. She told him less than half and spurred on. But horses tire, and courtesies do not, and when she raised the homestead at dusk his horse was already at the rail, and he sat on the porch in a dead man's hat, sipping coffee like he had grown there. Where is she, said Red. Inside, resting, said the drifter. Awful tired, your grandma. The door behind him opened on the twin barrels of grandmother's shotgun, held by grandmother, who had never rested a day in her life. The drifter set the coffee down with great care. Some houses, she said, you do not get to be the wolf in. Ride on.
