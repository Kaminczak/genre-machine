# Cinderella - Original Fairy Tale

**Logline:** A scullery maid is transformed by her fairy godmother. She loses a glass slipper at the prince's ball at midnight.

## Script

('Once upon a time there lived a girl whose mother had died, and whose father married again — a widow with two daughters of her own. They gave the girl the hearth to sleep by and the ashes for company, and the name Cinderella to remember her place. When the king announced a ball, the stepsisters left in silk, and Cinderella wept alone — until her fairy godmother appeared. A pumpkin became a carriage; mice became horses; rags became a gown of starlight, with slippers of pure glass. But the magic ends at midnight, the godmother warned. The prince danced with no one else all evening. At the last stroke of twelve Cinderella fled, losing one glass slipper on the palace stair. The prince searched the kingdom, trying the slipper on every foot. The stepsisters pushed and pinched in vain. Then Cinderella stepped from the kitchen, drew the second slipper from her apron, and the first fit her like morning fits the sun. She forgave her sisters — that is the part the tellings forget — and she and the prince lived happily, leaving the door of the palace kitchen open, always, for anyone who slept by a hearth.',)
