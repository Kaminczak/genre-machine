# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Original Fairy Tale

**Logline:** A golden-haired girl wanders into a cottage with three bowls of porridge, three chairs, three beds. The bears come home.

## Script

('Once upon a time, three bears lived in a house in the woods: a great big Papa Bear, a middle-sized Mama Bear, and a wee small Baby Bear. One morning their porridge was too hot, so they went for a walk while it cooled. Along came a girl with golden curls named Goldilocks, who knocked, and waited not at all, and went in. She tried the first porridge — too hot. The second — too cold. The third was just right, and she ate it all up. She tried the first chair — too hard. The second — too soft. The third was just right, and it broke to pieces beneath her. Upstairs she tried the beds — too high at the head, too high at the feet — until the little one was just right, and she fell fast asleep. Home came the bears. Someone has been eating my porridge, said Papa. Someone has been sitting in my chair, said Mama. Someone is still in my bed, said Baby Bear. Goldilocks woke, saw three bears, and went out the window at a speed never since equaled. The bears fixed the chair, and locked the door thereafter.',)
