# Little Red Riding Hood - Original Fairy Tale

**Logline:** A girl in a red hood crosses the woods with cakes for grandmother. The wolf reaches the cottage first and dresses in nightclothes.

## Script

Once upon a time there was a girl everyone called Little Red Riding Hood, for the crimson hood her grandmother had sewn her. One morning her mother packed a basket of cakes and said: go straight to grandmother's cottage, and do not stray from the path. In the woods a wolf fell into step beside her, asked politely where she was headed, and took the shortcut. When Red arrived, the cottage door stood open, and grandmother looked strange under her nightcap. What big ears you have, said Red. The better to hear you with. What big eyes you have. The better to see you with. What big teeth you have. The better to eat you with! But a woodsman heard the commotion, burst through the door, and drove the wolf out. Grandmother was freed from the wardrobe, the cakes were eaten, and Red never strayed from the path again.
