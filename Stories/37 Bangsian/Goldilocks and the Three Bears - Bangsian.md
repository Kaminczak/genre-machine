# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Bangsian

**Logline:** Goldilocks, deceased, drifts through the afterlife tasting other people's endings — too hot, too cold — while three bear philosophers debate whether 'just right' exists.

## Script

("The dead arrive in the Ursine district the way guests arrive everywhere: at mealtimes, unannounced. Goldilocks came through at ninety-six — the obituary said beloved grandmother, retired locksmith, and the afterlife found the second part hilarious — and made straight for the cottage she had been apologizing to for eighty-seven years. It stands in the philosophers' quarter now. The three bears, dead longer, have gone into ethics; it happens to everyone who spends eternity being asked about the one famous thing. Their table seats whoever is arguing that day: three bowls, eternally steaming, and around them the question that has outlived every principal — WAS the porridge hers to eat? The Great Bear holds for property: the bowls were his, the house was his, trespass is trespass in every world including this one. The Middle Bear holds for hospitality: a table set and a door unlocked is an offer in law and in love, and always was. And the Small Bear, who lost the chair and the porridge and the bed, holds — has held for two hundred years, to the despair of visiting Stoics — for hunger itself: that need outranks ownership anywhere the porridge is actually warm. They were mid-argument when she knocked. The room went quiet as a verdict. Then the Small Bear, delighted, pulled out the fourth chair he has kept mended since the day he died: at LAST. The witness. Sit. Eat. Settle nothing.",)
