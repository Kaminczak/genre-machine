# Three Little Pigs - Bangsian

**Logline:** Three deceased pigs debate architecture in the afterlife's garden suburbs — with Vitruvius, Socrates, and a wolf held up at the gates.

## Script

The afterlife's suburbs are zoned generously, eternity being long and the committee being dead. The three pigs arrived together — which is its own story, and they do not tell it — and were issued one plot and three opinions. Vitruvius came by on Thursdays to argue for brick, firmness, commodity, and delight, in that order. Socrates asked what a wall was for, and then asked it again, until the second pig hit him with a stick, which he agreed was one answer. The first pig raised straw out of habit, and the shade of a famous architect wept openly into the hedges. Then word came up from the gates: a wolf had arrived, winded, insisting he had died of perfectly natural causes, and demanding to be let in. The gatekeeper's dog — all three heads — declined to be convinced. The pigs conferred, as neighbors do, and voted to admit him. Even dead — especially dead — you keep the pot on. It is only polite. He asks, every dinner, how they did it. They ask him how he died. Nobody answers. It is a very good neighborhood.
