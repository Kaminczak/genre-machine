# Little Red Riding Hood - Bangsian

**Logline:** Red, long dead, retraces the path through an afterlife forest where every lost grandmother keeps a cottage — and the wolf waits at each door to apologize.

## Script

The dead arrive at the forest the way the living arrive at conclusions: gradually, then all at once. Red came through at ninety-one, hood folded in her hands, and found the afterlife's wood to be an orchard of cottages — every grandmother anyone ever lost keeps one, and the lamps go on at dusk in their thousands, and the smell of baking crosses denominational lines. Her own grandmother's cottage she knew by the crooked chimney, restored to its best year. But at every gate along the path stood the same figure, gray and patient: the wolf, making his rounds, apologizing door to door. He has been at it for centuries. The grandmothers receive him with the special mercilessness of hostesses: they accept the apology, again, and then they feed him, again, and send him on with a parcel, again, to the next gate. It is not punishment, exactly. It is worse; it is family. At her grandmother's gate Red waited her turn behind him. He finished, bowed, took his parcel. Your turn, dear, said grandmother, glowing. He is sorry. I am dead, said Red. So is everyone, said grandmother. The cakes are real. Come in.
