# Three Little Pigs - Supernatural Horror

**Logline:** Three pig siblings shelter in a straw hut, a lakeside cabin, and a hilltop manor in fog-bound Dread Hollow. A spectral wolf named Fenris rises from the mist.

## Script

In the fog-enshrouded village of Dread Hollow, three pig siblings sought sanctuary from an ancient curse. The youngest, Faelan, took refuge in a straw hut located near the haunted woods, where whispers of lost souls echoed. Nevan, the middle sibling, chose a stick cabin next to an eerie, still lake, rumored to hide vengeful spirits. The eldest, Morrigan, fortified herself in a brick manor atop a hill, overlooking a graveyard where shadows danced at midnight. One chilling night, a spectral wolf named Fenris, a spirit cursed to roam the land, emerged from the mists. Drawn to Faelan's hut, the ghostly howls terrified the young pig, sending him to Nevan's cabin. Fenris's haunting presence followed, driving both to seek Morrigan's protection. As Fenris loomed at the manor's gates, Morrigan chanted an old protective spell, dispelling the spirit and breaking the ancient curse, allowing peace to finally descend upon Dread Hollow.
