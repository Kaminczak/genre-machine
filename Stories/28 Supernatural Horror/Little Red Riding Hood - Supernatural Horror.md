# Little Red Riding Hood - Supernatural Horror

**Logline:** A girl in a red cloak walks a wood where the trees remember. Grandmother's cottage has been empty for forty years, and something inside is still wearing her voice.

## Script

The cottage has been empty for forty years. Everyone in the village knows this. They also know that smoke rises from its chimney on the cold nights, and that the smoke smells of cakes, and nobody discusses it. Red was not born when grandmother vanished; she knows her only as the photograph on the mantel and the voice in her mother's warnings: never the woods path, never after dusk, never answer if it calls you by name. It called her by name on the third of November. Red, from the treeline, in grandmother's voice, gentle as wool: I have been waiting, child. The basket is still here. She went. You always go; that is how these stories know themselves. The path was shorter than it should have been, the door open, the fire lit, and in the bed a shape of grandmother-warmth with its back turned, saying come in, come in out of the cold. What big hands you have, whispered Red, from the doorway. There was a long, wrong pause. The shape said: I had to carry so much, so far. Red left the basket on the step. Some doors you feed. You do not enter them.
