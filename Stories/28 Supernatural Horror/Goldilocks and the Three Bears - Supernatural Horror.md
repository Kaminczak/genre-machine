# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Supernatural Horror

**Logline:** A blonde girl shelters in a cabin where three of everything waits, still warm. The bears died in these rooms in 1911, and they are coming home anyway.

## Script

('The Ursine family died in that house in 1911 — the flu took all three inside of a week, biggest to smallest — and the house has kept their habits ever since. The village knows the rules: the porridge smell on cold mornings is not an invitation, the rocking on the porch is not a greeting, and you do not, whatever the weather, sleep there. The girl was from away, gold-haired, walking the coast road when the fog shut it. She knocked; the door was open; the table was laid. Three bowls, steaming, in a house with no fire lit — she was too cold to do that arithmetic. The first bowl burned her mouth with heat that had no source. The second was grave-cold. The third was just right, and tasted of a recipe nobody living remembers. The small chair gave beneath her without a sound, folding like it had been waiting to. The small bed received her the way water receives a stone. She woke — if woke is the word — to three shapes at the bedfoot, great and middle and small, patient as portraits. Someone has been eating my porridge, said the smallest, in a voice like a child heard through a wall, and someone can stay. The villagers still set the coast road sign each fall. There are four rocking chairs on the porch now. On cold mornings, the smell of porridge, and under it, faintly, laughter.',)
