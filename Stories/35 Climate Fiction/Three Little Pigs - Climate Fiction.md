# Three Little Pigs - Climate Fiction

**Logline:** The storm surge takes the coast one house at a time. Three pig siblings build for the world that's coming, not the one that's gone.

## Script

The insurance maps still called it a floodplain, as if the flood ever really left. Marsh built fast on the sand out of salvaged siding, because the projections said category twos, at worst, and Marsh believed projections. Reed built on pilings, fully code-compliant — certified against the storms of twenty years ago. Sedge built earthbag and cistern on the high ground and planted a line of mangroves the year nobody was planting anything, and the town called it a waste of a waterfront. The wolf was not an animal. That was just the name the season drew for the eleventh storm by June. It huffed the way water huffs, low and patient, and took Marsh's place in an afternoon. Reed's pilings held through the surge and lost to the wind. On the high ground, all three of them, the cistern full, the shutters singing, the mangroves bent flat and holding. Wolf circled for two days, looking for the way in. The way in had been planted shut ten years before.
