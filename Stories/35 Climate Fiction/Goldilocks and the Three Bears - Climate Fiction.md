# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Climate Fiction

**Logline:** After the thaw failed, the bears stopped hibernating. Goldilocks breaks in looking for food and finds three rationed bowls and a family as hungry as she is.

## Script

("The winter the bears stopped hibernating was the winter everything else stopped pretending. The thaw came in January, then the freeze, then the false spring that killed the berry crop in bud, and by August the Ursine family — the valley called them that, the three browns who ranged the old orchard — were awake, and thin, and figuring out doors. The girl found their den-house in September; hunger by then had walked her out past the county line and manners were a country she had emigrated from. Inside: the bears' cache, raided and re-hoarded from twenty ruined farms, portioned — this is the detail the biologists would not believe — into three heaps by size. The big cache was rot-riddled. The middle had frozen and split. The small was sound: windfall apples, honeycomb, a salvaged tin. She ate from the small pile, slept in the small hollow of dragged blankets, and woke ringed by three shapes who had learned, in the hungry year, exactly what she had learned: that the old rules were a climate that no longer existed. They did not charge. She did not run. In the new weather, calories are the only law, and she had left the tin. The rangers' report from that valley is one line long, and the line is: three bears and an unidentified minor are wintering in the Hollis barn, and provisioning appears to be mutual.",)
