# Three Little Pigs - Post-Apocalyptic

**Logline:** Three pigs shelter in plastic, rusted metal, and a concrete bunker in a ruined world. A war machine left over from the last conflict hunts survivors across the wastes.

## Script

In a bleak, post-apocalyptic world, three pigs sought refuge from the chaos. The first pig, in desperation, constructed a fragile shelter from discarded plastic. The second, scavenging from ruined cities, built a makeshift home of rusted metal. The third, foreseeing the dangers, fortified a bunker from concrete and steel. A monstrous machine, remnants of a past war, patrolled the wastelands, hunting survivors. It crushed the plastic shelter effortlessly, forcing the first pig to flee to the metal refuge. The machine, relentless, tore through the metal, driving the two pigs to the bunker. Its efforts to breach the bunker were futile. Attempting to infiltrate through an air vent, it was met with electrified defenses set up by the third pig. Defeated, the machine withdrew, leaving the pigs to endure in their fortress, a testament to foresight in a fallen world.
