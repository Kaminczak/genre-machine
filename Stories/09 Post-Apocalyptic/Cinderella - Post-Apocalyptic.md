# Cinderella - Post-Apocalyptic

**Logline:** A ruined-city scavenger is gifted scavenged finery by a wasteland witch. A scuffed glass shard tied to her foot drops in the warlord's stronghold at midnight.

## Script

("The settlement ran on salvage and seniority, and the girl had neither: last one taken in, first one sent into the ash-fields, sleeping by the burn barrel for the warmth the bunkhouse would not spare. When the warlord's stronghold announced a gathering — amnesty night, all camps welcome, alliances to be made — her step-kin took the truck and left her the graveyard shift. The witch found her there. Every wasteland has one: the old woman off the maps, who remembers the world before and trades in its artifacts. She dressed the girl out of a sealed footlocker — a dress from before, unfaded, smelling of a dead world's flowers — and tied to her feet the strangest salvage in her hoard: shards of glass, ground smooth, a shoe from before, its mate long gone, plus one built to match. The truce ends at midnight, the witch said. That is not magic. That is warlords. She walked into the stronghold like a rumor of the old world, and the warlord's son — who had grown up on scavenge and never seen anything that was not patched — could not look away. At midnight the truce flag came down and the shooting resumed on schedule, and she ran, and the built shoe stayed in the mud at the gate. He carries it on his belt now, they say, camp to camp, not trying it on feet — asking, instead, who remembers the old woman off the maps. Half the wasteland is walking on salvage. He is looking for the one who walked in glass.",)
