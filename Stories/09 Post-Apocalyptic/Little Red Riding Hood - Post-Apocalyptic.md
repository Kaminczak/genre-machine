# Little Red Riding Hood - Post-Apocalyptic

**Logline:** A girl in a faded red windbreaker scavenges through ruined suburbs to grandmother's shelter. The wolf is a mutant raider already inside.

## Script

The suburbs died twenty years back, but the street signs still try. Red moved down what was left of Maple with her hood cinched against the ash-wind, basket of purified water and protein cake for grandmother's shelter under the old library. The wolves out here walk on two legs mostly. The shelter hatch was unsealed. That was the first wrong thing. The second was the smell of someone else's fire. He sat in grandmother's chair by grandmother's stove, a raider with a scavenged gas mask pushed up on his scarred head, her tin cup in his hand. Big eyes, he said, mocking. All the better to have seen you coming, Red answered, and dropped the flare she had lit in the stairwell. The tunnel's old sprinkler line, the one grandmother kept charged with fuel instead of water, did what it was built to do. She dug grandmother out of the storm cellar an hour later. They drank the water. They let the library burn out above them, and counted it cheap.
