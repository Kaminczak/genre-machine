# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Post-Apocalyptic

**Logline:** A blonde scavenger picks through a pre-collapse cottage. Three cans — bloated, edible, perfect. The mutant Ursine clan returns.

## Script

("The house still had a roof, which put it in the top percentile of houses. The scavenger went in through the kitchen window, golden hair tucked under a rag, and froze: three cans on the table, opened, recent. One bulged — botulism, death in tin. One had frozen and thawed too many times, gone to gray sludge. The third was perfect: beans, factory-sealed till this morning, still warm from someone's fire. Every rule said walk away. Hunger has its own rules. She ate it standing, watching the door, then searched the place: three sleeping mats, three packs, three water jugs sized large, middle, small. She should have left. Instead she sat, just a minute, on the smallest mat, and twenty hours of walking took her like a wave. The Ursine clan found her asleep at dusk — the big one first, patched coat and patient eyes, then the other two. Mutation had taken their outline but left their arithmetic: three mats, three packs, one girl. Someone ate the good can, said the big one, to the room. The smallest crouched, considering her the way you consider a stray. They let her keep the mat that night. In the wasteland, that is either mercy or recruitment, and by morning she found she did not care which.",)
