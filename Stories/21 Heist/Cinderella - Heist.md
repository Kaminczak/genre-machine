# Cinderella - Heist

**Logline:** Crew: scullery infiltrator, godmother fixer, mice burglars. The mark: the prince's vault, opened only at midnight. Signature: a single glass slipper.

## Script

("The palace vault opened once a year, at midnight, on the night of the Royal Ball — a design flaw dressed as a tradition, which is the best kind. The crew was four: Marraine ran it, silver-haired, allegedly retired, actually bored; the twins handled locks and lifts; and the inside girl was her masterpiece — a scullery hire named Ella, two years deep in the palace's trust, invisible the way only staff are invisible. The ball was the cover; the gown was the uniform; the glass slippers were Marraine's signature, and her vanity, and the plan's one flourish: heels of annealed glass, hollow, sized for the take — the Margrave diamonds, one per heel. Ella danced with the prince because the prince would not stop asking, and because a girl dancing with the prince is the one person in the palace no guard watches. At 11:58 she was at the vault as it cycled; at 11:59 the diamonds were in her heels; at 12:00, the flaw in every plan — she liked him. Had liked him for three dances. So she did the unprofessional thing, the thing Marraine still tells at Christmas: she left him a heel on the stair. One diamond. Half the take, returned before it was missed, wrapped in glass, an apology and a confession in a size six. The palace called it the strangest attempted theft in its history: nothing missing but one diamond, and that one surrendered. The prince understood it correctly: as a message. He announced he would try the slipper on every foot in the kingdom, which even the guards knew made no sense. It was not about the fit. It was an invitation to negotiate. Marraine let her take the meeting. The wedding was at midnight. The vault schedule was changed, at the new princess's insistence, and the twins send regards every Christmas from somewhere warm.",)
