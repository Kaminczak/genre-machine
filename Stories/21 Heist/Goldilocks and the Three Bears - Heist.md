("The Ursine vault had three tiers, and the blonde had studied all of them from a rooftop across the street for a month. Tier one: thermal — the entry corridor ran hot as porridge fresh off the fire, cooking cheap electronics and impatient thieves. She went in at the cold hour before dawn with hardware rated for ovens.
