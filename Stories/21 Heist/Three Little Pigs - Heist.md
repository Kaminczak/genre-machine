# Three Little Pigs - Heist

**Logline:** Three pig specialists — driver, demolitions, safecracker — case three vault types. The wolf is the inside man.

## Script

The job was three vaults in one night. Marla drove. Deke handled demolitions. Sy, the eldest, cracked safes and never once rushed a dial. Vault one was a straw box, a sheet-metal cabinet with a lock you could open by asking politely. Deke had it in ninety seconds. Vault two was stick, a proper commercial unit, and it cost them nine minutes and most of the charge. Vault three was brick, a genuine hardplate monster with a relocking mechanism from an era that took theft personally, and Sy had been studying its schematics for two years. The inside man was a wolf named Renner. He gave up the straw job to the cops. He gave up the stick job. Then he sold them the brick job, because he wanted to be there when it opened. So Sy opened it. And Renner stepped inside. And Sy closed it.
