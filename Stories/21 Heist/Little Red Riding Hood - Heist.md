# Little Red Riding Hood - Heist

**Logline:** A cat-burglar in red infiltrates grandmother's mansion-vault for an heirloom. A rival thief in wolf-gray got there first.

## Script

The Marchmont heirloom: a ruby pendant, grandmother's by right, stolen off her wall forty years back and sitting ever since in the Vulpe mansion vault like a grudge with a hallmark. Red went in on a Tuesday, red catsuit because her grandmother had sewn it and you wear what your grandmother sews you. Down the chimney, naturally; the Vulpes never learned. The vault door hung open, which was insulting. A rival was already inside — wolf-gray suit, silk gloves, the pendant in his palm and the gall to bow. Big vault, he said. All the better to loot, my dear. The pendant is spoken for, said Red. So am I, said the wolf-gray man agreeably, by the police, in three countries. They fought the way professionals fight, which is to say quietly and mostly with leverage. He escaped through the wine cellar with the decoy she had let him win. Grandmother clasped the ruby on that evening and studied her reflection a long time. He sounds charming, she said. Bring him to dinner. Steal something nice.
