# Three Little Pigs - Dystopian

**Logline:** Three pig citizens build sanctioned dwellings under the Wolf State's gaze. Surveillance cameras blink in every wall.

## Script

Citizen 7-Straw was assigned a dwelling of regulation fiber. Citizen 7-Stick received a dwelling of regulation composite. Citizen 7-Brick, whose compliance score permitted it, was assigned masonry. All three dwellings contained the standard Wolf State aperture, one per wall, blinking amber, for the safety of the citizen. When the reduction order came, Fiber was cited for structural nonconformity and removed within the hour. Composite was cited the following morning. Both citizens relocated to Brick, which was permitted, as relocation requests had been pre-approved eleven years earlier by an office that no longer exists. The Wolf State applied pressure to the masonry for six days. The masonry did not file a response. On the seventh day an inspector entered by the flue, as inspectors do, and encountered a heat source that had not been declared. The apertures recorded everything. The apertures were, at that moment, the only witnesses who did not lie.
