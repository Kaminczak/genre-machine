# Little Red Riding Hood - Dystopian

**Logline:** Subject R-7 in regulation-red walks to grandmother's flat. Wolf-Officer 12 awaits, wearing grandmother's authorization badge.

## Script

Movement Permit R-7 authorizes one minor, hood color: regulation red, to transit Sector 12 for the purpose of: familial nutrition delivery. The Ministry reminds citizens that grandmothers are a legacy designation. At checkpoint four the officer's badge read WOLF-12 and his smile was regulation. Contents of basket? Cakes, said R-7. Cakes are contraband under the Sugar Directive, said Wolf-12, but I am feeling generous. Proceed. Grandmother's housing unit answered on the sixth knock, which was the code for wrong, three knocks being the code for right. Inside, Wolf-12 sat in grandmother's chair wearing grandmother's authorization lanyard, having proceeded faster. Your grandmother has been relocated for her safety, he said. I am your grandmother now. The apertures in the walls blinked amber. R-7 set down the basket, lifted the false bottom, and read aloud the document grandmother had hidden there years ago: Wolf-12's own relocation order, pre-signed, post-dated, patient. The apertures, which record everything, recorded everything. The Ministry thanks its citizens.
