# Cinderella - Dystopian

**Logline:** Citizen C-12, drudge-tier, is illicitly elevated. The state's identifier-slipper, glass-coded, falls at the Prince Bureau's annual selection gala.

## Script

("Citizen C-12, drudge-tier, Household 7, is reminded that tier is destiny and destiny is calibrated annually. The Selection Gala exists so that the Prince Bureau may elevate one citizen per year, pour encourager les autres; attendance is by merit badge, and C-12's badges were administratively absorbed by her step-guardian's daughters, a correction the Ministry declines to correct. The person who intercepted C-12 at the refuse chute on Gala night carried credentials that predate the Ministry and therefore, by a loophole no one has closed because no one admits it exists, supersede it. She issued: one gown, gray-market, woven of decommissioned flags; one transit pass, pumpkin-class, self-driving; two slippers of glass — regulation transparent, the Ministry approves of transparency — containing, engraved at the heel where only a reader would find it, C-12's original, unabsorbed merit record. The elevation protocol requires the Prince Bureau to dance with candidates until identity is confirmed. Confirmation was interrupted at 0000 by curfew, which outranks the Bureau. C-12 fled as required. The slipper remained, as evidence does. The Bureau's audit of one glass slipper took eleven months, exhumed the absorbed badges, elevated C-12, and demoted Household 7 in a single order. The Ministry wishes to clarify that this outcome demonstrates the system functioning as designed. The Ministry has always wished to clarify this. The other slipper has not been surrendered. The Ministry is looking for it.",)
