# Little Red Riding Hood - Mythic Epic

**Logline:** A maiden in sacred crimson crosses the threshold-forest with offerings to a forest crone. A wolf-spirit claims the offering — and the maiden.

## Script

Sing now of the maiden in the crimson veil, last daughter of the hearth-line, who carried offerings through the threshold-forest where the living do not linger. Honey she bore, and barley-cake, and salt, for the Crone of the wood's dark heart, who had been her grandmother before grandmothers were needed, in the time before the wood was cut from the world. At the black river a wolf-spirit rose against her, ancient, jealous, robed in famine, and demanded the offerings, and the maiden, and her name into the bargain. The offerings are not mine to give, said the maiden, nor am I. The wolf-spirit laughed and swallowed her whole, veil and basket and all, which is what the Crone had counted on. For the crimson veil was woven of dawn, and dawn cannot be kept down. The wolf-spirit split like a husk at the river's edge, and out of him walked the maiden, and out of her shadow walked all the swallowed dead he had ever taken, single file, blinking. The Crone met them with bread. The wood keeps the wolf's pelt as a rug, and remembers.
