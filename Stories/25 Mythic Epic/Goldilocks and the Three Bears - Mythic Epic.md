# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Mythic Epic

**Logline:** A golden-haired maiden trespasses in the grove-shrine of three bear-spirits. Three altars, three thrones of carved stone, three biers. The bear-spirits descend with thunder.

## Script

("Hear now the trespass of the golden one, in the grove where mortals do not eat. Three altars stood in the Ursine wood, raised before the naming of rivers: the Great Altar steaming with the first portion, the Middle cold with the portion of memory, the Small bearing the portion of the young god, sweet and just. The golden one came hungry out of the mortal lands, and hunger has no theology; she ate the young god's portion to the last grain. She sat upon the Small Throne, and it, being carved for a god's lightness and not a mortal's weight of years and sorrow, split to its root. She slept then on the Small Bier, where the young god dreams the world's spring, and her mortal dreams got into the weave of it, which is why, the singers say, spring has ever after come with mud and longing mixed into its glory. The three descended at dusk with thunder on their shoulders. Who has eaten of the offering? spoke the Great, and mountains heard. But the young god looked long at the sleeping thief, at the mended-world crookedness of her, and spoke the words that changed the law: she was hungry, and it was food. Let the grove keep a fourth portion evermore, for whoever comes golden and starving out of the dark. And it is kept. It is kept still.",)
