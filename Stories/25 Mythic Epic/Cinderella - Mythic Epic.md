# Cinderella - Mythic Epic

**Logline:** A girl of hearth-ash, daughter of a vanished king, is gifted one night by an underworld goddess. She leaves a glass sandal at the dawn-king's threshold.

## Script

("Sing, muse, of the ash-daughter, whom the hearth kept when her mother's line was scattered — for her mother had been beloved of the goddess of the hearth, and the hearth does not forget its debts though mortals do. The stepmother set her among cinders to erase her; but ash, the poets know, is what remains when fire has proven a thing true. When the king's son held his choosing-feast, the gods convened as they do at mortal thresholds, and the hearth-goddess claimed her right of gift: she robed the girl in cloth of banked embers, gray until it moved and then all gold, and shod her in glass — sand made honest by fire, the hearth's own element, the only material the gods make from punishment. Walk in these, the goddess said, and every step declares what you have endured and what it made of you. The prince knew her for what she was, not who; that is the correct order. But mortals feast past their allotments, and at midnight the goddess's gift returned to her element, as gifts of fire do. The girl fled shining and diminishing, and on the palace stair one slipper held its shape — for it had touched the threshold-stone, and thresholds are sacred, and what stands on the sacred keeps its form. Every maiden of the kingdom set her foot to it, and it burned the false ones, not cruelly, but as truth burns, with instruction. When the ash-daughter's foot slid home the slipper sang — a hearth-note, the sound of a fire settling into a long night, the sound houses make when someone has finally come home. She reigned with dust on her hands, and no hearth in that kingdom ever went cold in her lifetime, and the poets end her song not with the wedding but with the law she made: that every house owes its warmth first to whoever tends it. Thus the hearth. Thus its daughter. Thus the song.",)
