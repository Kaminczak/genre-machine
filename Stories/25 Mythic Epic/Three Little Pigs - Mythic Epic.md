# Three Little Pigs - Mythic Epic

**Logline:** Three pig demigods raise temples of reed, oak, and quarried marble. A wolf-god, jealous of mortal pride, descends from the storm-summit.

## Script

Hear now of the three sons of the Sow-Mother, who were given breath and pride in equal measure. The youngest raised a temple of river reed and named it after the morning. The second hewed oak from the sacred grove and named his temple after his own hands, which was his first error. The eldest quarried marble from the throat of the mountain and set it without a name at all, for he had heard what happens to the named. Fenris-of-the-Storm-Summit, a god who had never been mortal and resented the arrangement, came down the sky in jealousy. Reed he scattered as breath scatters chaff. Oak he tore, and the grove wept. But the unnamed marble he could not move, for it claimed nothing and so offered nothing to break. He entered by the smoke-hole, as gods do when the door refuses them, and found there the one fire that has always outlasted pride.
