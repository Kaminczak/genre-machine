# Cinderella - 1950s Suburban Mystery

**Logline:** A blonde teen babysits for the wealthy Charmings across town. After the Country Club gala, her glass pump is found — and the polite suburb stops being polite.

## Script

("The Charmings' Country Club gala was the event of the season in Fairview Estates, and everyone who was anyone was there, which is precisely how everyone missed it. The Pemberton widow arrived with her two daughters, poured into taffeta, and spent the evening steering them at the Charming boy like tugboats docking a liner. Nobody recognized the girl in the borrowed blue gown because nobody had ever looked at her: the Pembertons' live-in help, the late Mr. Pemberton's actual daughter, a fact filed under things Fairview does not discuss, along with where the first Mrs. Pemberton's jewelry went. She danced with the Charming boy from nine to midnight and told him the truth, which in Fairview is an exotic accent. Then she was gone, and on the clubhouse steps: one glass pump, size six, the mate of a pair the first Mrs. Pemberton wears in her portrait. The gossip columns called it a mystery. The Charming boy called it evidence. He knocked on every door in the Estates with the shoe in a hatbox, and the neighborhood performed not knowing whose it was with a unanimity that told him everything. It fit the Pemberton help, in the Pemberton kitchen, under the Pemberton widow's frozen smile. The wedding was at the club. The columns covered the dress. What Fairview does not discuss, to this day, is the exhumed will, the jewelry, and why the widow sold at a loss in the spring. The pumps are in the portrait now. Both of them. The new Mrs. Charming had it repainted.",)
