# Little Red Riding Hood - 1950s Suburban Mystery

**Logline:** A girl in a red Sunday coat walks two suburban blocks to grandmother's split-level. The wolf is the milkman — who hasn't been the milkman for some time.

## Script

The Herald-Gazette, Thursday edition: MILKMAN ROUTE CHANGES ANNOUNCED. Nobody read past the headline, which was a shame. Red walked the two suburban blocks to grandmother's split-level every Sunday, red coat buttoned, casserole in the basket, past lawns so even they looked ironed. The milkman waved from his truck at the corner of Elm. He had grandmother's street on his route now. He asked, pleasantly, what time the old dear usually napped. On Sunday the milk bottles on grandmother's step stood unemptied, and the door chimed open before Red touched it. The living room was immaculate. The television murmured. The figure in grandmother's recliner wore her reading glasses and had folded its hands in an attitude of naps. You are not the milkman, Red said. The milkman retired in March. It has been very easy, said the figure, not moving. This neighborhood. Nobody looks at anything. Red smiled her Sunday smile and reached into the basket, where the casserole dish held what the Neighborhood Watch, whom she had telephoned from the corner, called the evidence.
