# Three Little Pigs - 1950s Suburban Mystery

**Logline:** Three pig families move into identical pastel ranches on Wolfsbane Lane. Something hollows out the brickwork from inside.

## Script

Wolfsbane Lane went up in a single season: eleven identical ranch homes, mint, buttercup, and dusty rose. The Strand family took the mint one, prefab siding, quick and cheerful. The Wicketts took buttercup, honest frame construction. The Brickmans took dusty rose at the end of the cul-de-sac, double-course masonry, and the neighbors thought it was a bit much. In March the Strands' siding began to sound hollow when you knocked. In April it was not there at all, and neither were the Strands. In May the Wicketts' frame gave the same hollow note, three days before the moving truck nobody called arrived. By June every family on the lane was in the dusty rose house at the end, and something spent the whole summer testing the masonry from outside, patiently, at night. The Brickmans kept the fireplace lit through July. Nobody on Wolfsbane Lane ever asked them why.
