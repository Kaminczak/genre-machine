# Little Red Riding Hood - Silent Film

**Logline:** Iris on a girl in a black-and-white-but-clearly-red cloak. Wolf mugs at camera, dons cap, leaps into bed.

## Script

IRIS OPENS on a girl in a cloak that reads red even in black and white, skipping down a painted forest path, basket swinging with catastrophic confidence. TITLE CARD: TO GRANDMOTHER'S HOUSE — NO DAWDLING! Enter WOLF, tiptoeing tree to tree in perfect view of everyone but her, twirling his mustache. He points ahead, mimes a shortcut, and sprints off screen left, colliding with the frame edge once for good measure. INTERIOR: COTTAGE. The wolf dons cap and gown, leaps into bed, and arranges the blanket with dainty little pats. Red enters. She looks at the wolf. She looks at the camera. She looks at the wolf. TITLE CARD: MY, GRANDMOTHER — WHAT BIG EVERYTHING! The wolf's ears wilt. The wardrobe door swings open and GRANDMOTHER emerges with a rolling pin, cranked to double speed. A chase: around the bed, through the bed, briefly inside the wallpaper. IRIS CLOSES on the wolf running for the horizon wearing the nightcap, and two ladies sharing cake.
