# Three Little Pigs - Silent Film

**Logline:** Iris opens on three pigs building tiny dwellings, mugging at the camera. Wolf mimes a knock; the door explodes in splinters.

## Script

IRIS OPENS. Three pigs, three bowler hats, three tiny houses, all mugging shamelessly at the camera. TITLE CARD: BROTHER THE FIRST BUILDS OF STRAW. He builds it in four seconds and takes a bow. TITLE CARD: BROTHER THE SECOND PREFERS STICKS. He hammers his thumb. He hops. The audience roars. TITLE CARD: BROTHER THE THIRD LAYS BRICK, AND WILL NOT BE HURRIED. The others point and laugh in enormous pantomime. Enter WOLF, twirling a mustache that is frankly too large. He mimes a polite knock at the straw door. The straw door explodes into confetti. He knocks at the sticks. The sticks become a dust cloud with legs in it. He knocks at the brick. The brick does not move, and the wolf's hat falls off, and he stares directly into the lens. TITLE CARD: HE TRIES THE CHIMNEY. IRIS CLOSES on a pot, and a splash, and a hat.
