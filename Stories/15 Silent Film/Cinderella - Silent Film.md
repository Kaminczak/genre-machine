# Cinderella - Silent Film

**Logline:** Iris on weeping girl by hearth. Godmother waves wand. Iris on dance with prince. Clock strikes. Iris on lone glass slipper.

## Script

("IRIS OPENS on a hearth, and a girl asleep against it, cheek striped with ash. Above her, framed, a portrait of her father; the frame is draped in black. Enter the STEPSISTERS in curl-papers, pantomiming a ball with invisible partners, waving an invitation the size of a door. They exit trailing feathers. TITLE CARD: EVERYONE IS INVITED. NEARLY EVERYONE. The girl sweeps. She sweeps the same spot until the broom wears a groove, and the audience is not laughing now. ENTER GODMOTHER, in a flash-pot of smoke, dusting herself, checking a pocket watch bigger than her head. She circles the girl, appraising, then rolls up her sleeves with enormous ceremony. THE PUMPKIN GAG: it swells, it trembles, it becomes a carriage, and one mouse in a coachman's hat takes a bow and falls off the seat. THE DRESS: the godmother spins the girl like a top; she blurs; she stops, gowned, and the ashes rise off her in a little cloud shaped like a heart. TITLE CARD: MIDNIGHT, MIND. THE WATCH IS NOT A SUGGESTION. THE BALL: the prince and the girl dance while the stepsisters' invisible partners abandon them visibly. THE CLOCK: enormous, filling the frame, hands sprinting. She runs — down steps that multiply as the camera cranks — and one slipper stays, center frame, catching the light. IRIS CLOSES to a pinpoint on the slipper. Then opens once more, small: the prince, holding it to his chest. TITLE CARD: SHE LEFT AT MIDNIGHT. HE BEGAN AT DAWN.",)
