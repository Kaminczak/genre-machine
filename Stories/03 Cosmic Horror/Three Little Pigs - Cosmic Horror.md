# Three Little Pigs - Cosmic Horror

**Logline:** Three pigs huddle in shrines of straw, bone, and basalt. The wolf is no animal — it is a hunger between stars.

## Script

They were not houses. They were shrines. The youngest raised his of straw and dried marsh-reed and called it prayer. The second built of bone gathered from a shore that had never held a sea. The eldest cut basalt from the black hill and set every stone against an angle that hurt to look at. The thing they feared was not a wolf. It wore the word wolf the way a hand wears a glove. It came down from between the stars with a hunger older than appetite, and the straw shrine did not fall so much as forget it had ever stood. The bone shrine unmade itself in reverse. At the basalt door the hunger pressed, and pressed, and something in the geometry refused. It sought the flue instead. Below waited a fire the eldest had kept burning for eleven years without once explaining why.
