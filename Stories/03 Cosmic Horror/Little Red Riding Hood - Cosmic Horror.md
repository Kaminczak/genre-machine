# Little Red Riding Hood - Cosmic Horror

**Logline:** A girl in a blood-red cloak walks the petrified forest. Grandmother is already there, smiling with too many teeth.

## Script

The forest had been trees once, before it learned stillness. Red walked the petrified rows with her basket, and the stone trunks leaned very slightly toward her as she passed, the way sunflowers follow light, if light could be eaten. Grandmother's cottage stood where it always stood, but the windows were wrong: they showed the inside of a house much larger than the outside. Grandmother was already there. Grandmother was always already there. She sat in the bed smiling, and the smile continued past the point where smiles stop. What big eyes, Red whispered, because the words were carved over the door and had to be said. Eyes came the answer, from somewhere behind the ceiling, we have never needed eyes. The thing wearing grandmother folded back the quilt. Red did not run. Running assumes a direction, and the path behind her had been quietly rolled up like a tongue. The basket was accepted. Something was accepted. The woods lean listening still.
