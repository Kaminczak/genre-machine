# Cinderella - Cosmic Horror

**Logline:** A ragged servant girl is transformed by an entity that called itself her godmother. She leaves behind a slipper of bone-glass.

## Script

("The thing that came when the girl wept by the hearth was not her godmother, though it wore godmother the way a hand wears a glove. It arrived from a direction the room did not have and offered kindness, which is how you know, in that country, to be afraid. It made the pumpkin into a carriage, but the carriage's angles hurt to count. It made the mice into horses that were only horses if you did not look at their shadows. The gown it spun was beautiful; the seamstresses at the palace later went mad trying to name the color. Dance, it said, and be seen. Being seen was the price. Every eye at the ball fed something through her; she felt it drink. The prince asked her name and she found she was no longer certain. At midnight — and this is the mercy in it, midnight was the mercy — the working snapped, and she ran, and what she left on the stair was not a slipper but the part of her the glass had been. The prince's men still carry it door to door. It fits everyone now. That is the trouble. It fits everyone.",)
