# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Cosmic Horror

**Logline:** A pale girl crosses the threshold of a cabin where three of everything sits — yet a fourth chair, fourth bowl, fourth bed wait. The bears are not bears.

## Script

('The cabin stood where the survey said no cabin stood, and inside, everything came in threes. Three bowls on the table, steam rising from two. Three chairs facing the hearth. Three beds under the eave. The girl with golden hair counted them twice, because on the second count there were four. A fourth bowl, waiting. A fourth chair, angled toward her. A fourth bed, turned down. The porridge in the first bowl was too hot in a way that had nothing to do with temperature. The second was cold the way the space between stars is cold. The third was just right, and that was the worst of all, because it was exactly, precisely right — salted for her tongue, cooled for her mouth, waiting for her hunger. The bears came home at dusk, if they were bears. They stood in the doorway wearing their shapes politely, the way one wears borrowed clothes. Someone has been eating from the fourth bowl, said the largest, and its voice arrived a half-second before its mouth moved. We set it out so long ago. We had almost stopped hoping.',)
