# Three Little Pigs - Slapstick Cartoon

**Logline:** Three pig brothers in bowler hats hammer houses that collapse on contact. A tuxedoed wolf huffs himself into orbit.

## Script

Bowler hat number one! Straw house! UP in three seconds! DOWN in two! Bowler hat number two! Stick house! He hammers a nail, the wall falls on him, he pops out the other side flat as a pancake and inflates himself with a bicycle pump! Bowler hat number three lays brick with a tiny trowel and a tiny frown and takes ALL DAY, and his brothers laugh so hard their hats spin. Enter the wolf. Tuxedo. Top hat. Monocle, for some reason. He inhales the entire sky. He blows. The straw house becomes weather. He inhales again. The stick house becomes a cloud of question marks. He inhales a THIRD time, aims at the brick, blows with everything he has, and launches HIMSELF straight up through six clouds and one very surprised bird. He lands in the chimney. There is a pot. There is a splash. There is a wolf-shaped hole in the horizon.
