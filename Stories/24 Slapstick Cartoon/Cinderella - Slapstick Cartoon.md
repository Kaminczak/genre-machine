# Cinderella - Slapstick Cartoon

**Logline:** A scrubbing girl is whacked into a ballgown. Mice become a coach that breaks. A glass slipper bounces and refuses to fit nine ugly stepfeet.

## Script

("The invitation hits the doormat like a meteor — BALL TONIGHT — and the stepsisters stampede over Cinderella in a cloud of curlers and elbows, flattening her into the hallway rug, pattern and all. She peels herself off, sighs, accordion-folds back into shape. In the kitchen, her tears water a pumpkin, which grows interested. POOF: the Fairy Godmother arrives via chimney, upside down, checks her wand like a golfer, and gets to work. She zaps the pumpkin: it becomes a carriage, then a squash, then a carriage, then briefly a school bus, then a carriage with a squash's attitude. She zaps the mice: four become horses; the fifth becomes a horse-sized mouse and is promoted to coachman on the spot. She zaps Cinderella, who spins like a drill bit through three outfits — deep-sea diver, beekeeper, matador — before the gown lands, sparkling, and two glass slippers drop from nowhere and ring like little bells. MIDNIGHT, the godmother mouths enormously, tapping a wristwatch the size of a skillet. THE BALL: Cinderella and the prince waltz; the stepsisters pursue in hopping fury, permanently one dance-step behind, colliding with waiters, a tuba, each other. THE CLOCK STRIKES: each BONG inflates the clock tower like a bullfrog. Cinderella bolts — gown pixelating back to patches behind her like a bad connection — hits the palace stairs, and runs clean out of one slipper, through the garden, over the wall, and into the horizon with the mouse-coachman pedaling the re-squashed pumpkin like a unicycle. The prince lifts the slipper. Hearts orbit his head. He points down the road with enormous decision. TITLE CARD: EVERY FOOT IN THE KINGDOM. The stepsisters, at home, are already sawing at their heels with nail files. The slipper, on its velvet cushion, looks at the camera and gulps. THAT'S ALL — no, wait. IT FITS. THAT'S ALL, FOLKS.",)
