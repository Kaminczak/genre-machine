# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Slapstick Cartoon

**Logline:** A blonde girl in pigtails bounces into a cottage. Three bowls catch fire, three chairs explode, three beds launch her through the roof. Three bears in nightcaps blink.

## Script

("The cottage door says OUT TO LUNCH and the O's have little bees in them. Enter GOLDILOCKS at a sprint, pursued by her own curiosity, which is drawn as a small tornado. PORRIDGE NUMBER ONE: she takes a spoonful and her pigtails launch like bottle rockets, steam-whistle, her face does a kettle. PORRIDGE NUMBER TWO: one spoonful and she flash-freezes into an ice cube with eyes; a passing penguin tips its hat. PORRIDGE NUMBER THREE: angelic choir, sunbeam from nowhere, she inhales it bowl and all. The bowl clangs off her teeth like a gong. CHAIRS: the big one ejects her, boing; the middle one swallows her whole, gulp; the small one holds for exactly one dignified second, then achieves its final form: toothpicks. Three sets of eyebrows rise in ascending order. SOMEONE'S BEEN EATING MY PORRIDGE — the voice rattles the picture frames. Goldilocks wakes mid-Z, gulps it back down, and exits through the roof leaving a Goldilocks-shaped skylight. The bears peer up through it. Rain begins. The smallest bear opens exactly one umbrella.
