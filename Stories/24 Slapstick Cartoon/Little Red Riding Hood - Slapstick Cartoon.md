# Little Red Riding Hood - Slapstick Cartoon

**Logline:** A girl in a red hood bumbles down a path dropping cakes. A tuxedoed wolf disguises in a wig that keeps falling off. Anvils ensue.

## Script

A girl skips down a forest path — boing, boing, boing — dropping exactly one cake per boing. Behind her, a wolf in a trench coat tiptoes from tree to tree, except every tree he hides behind is thinner than he is. He straps on a granny wig. The wig falls off. He nails it on. The nail bends. He takes the shortcut, marked SHORTCUT in letters that light up, and arrives at the cottage in a cloud of cartoon dust that files in behind him and sits down. Costume change! Nightgown, cap, spectacles, wig — the wig slides off and he catches it with a foot while making tea with both hands and one ear. Red arrives. My, grandmother, she says, checking her list, what big EVERYTHING you have. The wolf preens. The wig falls off. A pause the length of an orchestra taking a breath. From the wardrobe, GRANDMOTHER, wearing a catcher's mask, winds up with a rolling pin the size of a telephone pole. The cottage bulges once, rings like a bell, and ejects one wolf through the chimney, nightgown and all. The wig lands last, on the horizon, with a tiny puff.
