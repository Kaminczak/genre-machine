# Three Little Pigs - Detective Mystery

**Logline:** Three pig detectives in Whispering Pines work a locket that makes people vanish. A shadowy wolf named Silas brought it to their door.

## Script

In the enigmatic town of Whispering Pines, three pig detectives were renowned for solving the most perplexing cases. The youngest, Ivy, operated from a straw-covered detective agency, specializing in deciphering cryptic messages. Basil, the middle sibling, ran his investigations from a stick-built library, collecting and analyzing clues from ancient texts. The eldest, Marcellus, managed a grand brick police headquarters, coordinating with local authorities on high-profile cases. One misty morning, a shadowy wolf named Silas arrived with a mysterious locket, claiming it held the key to a hidden treasure. But every time someone tried to open it, they disappeared. Intrigued, Ivy worked on the locket's engravings, Basil researched its origins, and Marcellus organized a search for the missing individuals. Together, they unraveled a tale of intrigue, betrayal, and a hidden society, proving that some mysteries span generations.
