# Little Red Riding Hood - Detective Mystery

**Logline:** A teen sleuth in red follows clues through misty woods. Grandmother is missing. The wolf insists he is innocent from behind bars.

## Script

The Pinewood Gazette called it the Cottage Case, and the police called it closed, which is exactly why Red reopened it. A wolf sat in the county jail insisting he was innocent, and the thing that bothered her was the door: no forced entry, and grandmother locked up always. Missing: one grandmother. Found at the scene: a nightcap folded neatly — grandmother never folded — and flour on a windowsill in a kitchen grandmother never baked in. Red walked the path with her notebook. Flowers freshly picked along the verge, but grandmother was allergic. A neighbor who suddenly could afford a new fence. A woodsman whose axe was too clean. The wolf, interviewed through the bars, said only: I came for the interview she promised me. I am a journalist. Check my satchel. The satchel, logged into evidence and never opened, held press credentials and a half-written story exposing the Pinewood Land Company's plot to condemn the woods — signed by its source. Grandmother. Who walked out of the company's basement two days later, cross, hungry, and holding the deed. The fence, Red noted, came down that winter.
