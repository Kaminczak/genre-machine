# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Detective Mystery

**Logline:** A teen sleuth investigates the abandoned Ursine cabin. Three meals interrupted. Three chairs of varying intactness. Who left first — and who never left?

## Script

("The Ursine cabin had been empty for a week when the neighbors finally called it in, and the teen detective got there before the county did, notebook out, bicycle ditched in the ferns. Front door: unlocked, no damage. Table: three bowls, and here was the first oddity — the large one full and furred with mold, the middle full likewise, the small one scraped clean and washed. Who breaks in and does the dishes? Chair: broken, but the pieces stacked neatly by the hearth with a repair sketched on butcher paper in a child's hand. Beds: two made, one slept-in, and under its pillow a note that made her sit down on the floor and reread it twice. It is not a burglary if it is your own house. The county file says the Ursines had a daughter before the boy — golden-haired, fostered out in the lean year, the paperwork lost when the courthouse flooded. The bowls, the chair, the bed: not vandalism. Inventory. A girl measuring the childhood that went on without her, eating one meal in it, sleeping one night, fixing what she broke. The detective closed her notebook and did the only honest thing: she left the note where it lay and told the county it was raccoons. Some cases you solve. Some you protect.",)
