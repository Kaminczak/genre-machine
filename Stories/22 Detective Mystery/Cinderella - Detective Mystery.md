# Cinderella - Detective Mystery

**Logline:** After a gala, a glass slipper is found on the palace steps. A detective traces it through stepmothers, ash-stained hearths, and a missing girl.

## Script

("The palace called it a lost item; the detective, retained quietly by the Queen, called it what it was: the only witness to an identity. One glass slipper, abandoned mid-stair at midnight, its owner fled with a thoroughness that itself was a clue — no carriage hired, no gate log, no name on the list matching the woman half the ballroom described and no two described alike. The slipper knew things, though, to anyone who let it talk. Hand-ground glass, old technique, a provincial workshop that had closed twenty years back: an heirloom, then, not a purchase — a dead woman's shoe on a living woman's foot. The wear said its owner walked far and stood long: staff, not gentry. The size said young. The left-only scuffing said she habitually carried loads on her right hip — trays, buckets. A servant in her mother's slippers, at a ball she was not invited to, gone before the clocks finished striking: the detective had the shape of her by dawn and the address by Tuesday, because only three houses in the capital had buried a mistress twenty years back whose estate listed glass slippers, provincial make, pair. At the third house, a stepmother said no one here answers that description, in the too-fast way that is a confession with the grammar changed. The detective did not try the shoe on anyone. She simply asked, at the kitchen door, the question no one else had thought to ask the whole kingdom over: madam, you left in such a hurry — did you want it back? And the girl at the hearth stood, and said the thing that closed the case and opened everything else: I want what goes with it. The palace announced an engagement that spring. The detective declined the honors list. The fee, she said, had been the look on the stepmother's face, and it had already cleared.",)
