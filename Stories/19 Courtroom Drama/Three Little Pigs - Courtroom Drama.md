# Three Little Pigs - Courtroom Drama

**Logline:** Three pig plaintiffs sue the Wolf for property damage. Exhibits: ashes of straw, splinters of stick, intact brick. The Wolf takes the stand.

## Script

Case number 3-P-1843. Three plaintiffs, one defendant, four exhibits. Exhibit A: a paper bag of ash, formerly a straw dwelling. Exhibit B: a box of splinters, formerly a stick dwelling. Exhibit C: photographs of a brick dwelling, undamaged, load-bearing walls intact. Exhibit D: the defendant's own medical records, documenting a lung capacity the plaintiffs' counsel described as, quote, an instrument. The defendant took the stand against advice. He testified that he had merely knocked. Asked to explain Exhibit A, he said the straw was substandard. Asked to explain Exhibit B, he said the sticks were substandard. Asked why he had climbed onto the roof of Exhibit C at two in the morning, the defendant requested a recess. The jury deliberated for forty minutes. The verdict turned not on the ash or the splinters, but on a simple question the eldest plaintiff asked from the stand: then why is my house still standing?
