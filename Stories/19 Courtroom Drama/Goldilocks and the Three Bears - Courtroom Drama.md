# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Courtroom Drama

**Logline:** The Bear family v. Goldilocks: trespass, vandalism, consumption of porridge. Three witnesses. The defendant takes the stand.

## Script

("Cause 3-B-1844, Ursine et famille versus the minor known as Goldilocks. Charges: unlawful entry, conversion of porridge, destruction of one chair, unauthorized occupation of a bed. The defense conceded the facts and argued condition: the door, counsel noted, was unlocked, the porridge abandoned, the house, in law, an attractive nuisance. The prosecution called Ursine père, who testified with dignity to the temperature gradient of the household's breakfast, and Ursine mère, who produced the chair in exhibit, in a bag, in pieces. The turning testimony came from Ursine fils, aged nine, who when asked what the defendant had taken from him, considered the question with a gravity the courtroom later described as unbearable, and answered: nothing that will not mend, except I would have shared the porridge if she had knocked. The jury was out eleven minutes. Verdict: guilty on all counts; sentence, suspended in full, conditional on restitution — one chair, built by her own hands to a standard acceptable to the smallest plaintiff. The chair she delivered that autumn sits in the Ursine kitchen to this day. It is, the family stipulates, just right, and there are four chairs at the table now, and the door is still unlocked.",)
