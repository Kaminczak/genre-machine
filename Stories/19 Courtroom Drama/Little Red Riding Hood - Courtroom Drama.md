# Little Red Riding Hood - Courtroom Drama

**Logline:** The Wolf stands trial for impersonation, identity theft, and aggravated grandmotherhood. The girl in red, sole witness, takes the stand.

## Script

Case 7-R-1893, the State versus Wolfe. The charges: unlawful entry, impersonation of a grandmother, and attempted consumption in the first degree. The defense opened badly: my client was invited, counsel said, and the gallery has not stopped repeating it since. The prosecution called one witness. She wore red. She described the path, the flowers, the voice from the bed, in a level tone that the court reporter later called the calmest thing I ever transcribed. Then the famous exchange. And what did you observe about the defendant's eyes? That they were large. His ears? Large. His teeth? Counsel objected — leading — and was overruled, and the witness said, quietly: large, and pointed at me. The defendant thereupon rose and explained that the ears, eyes, and teeth were all the better to hear, see, and greet the witness with, which is when his own counsel requested a recess, a mistrial, and finally a different client. The jury was out nineteen minutes. The grandmother, recovered alive from the wardrobe, attended sentencing in her nightcap, and applauded.
