# Three Little Pigs - Cold War Spy

**Logline:** Three pig agents run safehouses in three sectors of divided Berlin. A wolf-coded defector turns out to be the asset.

## Script

Three safehouses. Three sectors. One asset nobody vetted properly. HAYLOFT operated out of a rented flat in the French sector with a single door and no contingency. TIMBER ran a warehouse in the British sector, two exits, adequate paperwork. MASONRY held a pre-war building in the American sector with reinforced walls, three routes out, and a stove that was never allowed to go cold. The defector was coded WOLF. He gave up HAYLOFT in eleven minutes, and the flat was empty before the boots hit the stairs. He gave up TIMBER the next morning. Both officers went to ground at MASONRY. There WOLF's handlers pushed for six weeks against a building that had already survived a war, and it declined to notice. The final approach came down through the ventilation shaft on a Tuesday. The stove was lit. It had been lit since 1961. That was the entire point of the stove.
