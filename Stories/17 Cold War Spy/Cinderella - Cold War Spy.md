# Cinderella - Cold War Spy

**Logline:** Asset Cinder is given a gown and dossier for one embassy ball. Her glass heel — listening-device — left at the prince-handler's residence at midnight.

## Script

("The embassy ball was the year's one soft target: every attaché, every handler, every open secret in formal wear. Our asset — cryptonym CINDER — worked scullery in the Ambassador's residence, placed there two years prior and forgotten by everyone including, officially, us. The order came at 1800: activate, insert, retrieve the Wolfhound list from the Ambassador's study, exfiltrate by 0000 when the residence gates sealed. Materials were furnished by the officer she knew only as MARRAINE — a legend among assets, believed retired, believed dead, believed several contradictory things, all useful. Gown: Paris label, lining stitched with lockpicks. Transport: a gray sedan, requisition code PUMPKIN. Footwear: glass heels, and in the left one, a wire recorder — glass, MARRAINE noted, passes every metal detector the Ministry has bought. CINDER entered on the arm of a manufactured invitation and danced twice with the Ambassador's son, who talked, as principals' sons do, about everything his father hated: including the study, its clock, its habits. At 2340 she had the list photographed. At 2358 she was on the stairs when the son caught her wrist — not suspicion, the debrief insists; sentiment. She left him the right heel, empty, a souvenir for the counterintelligence men to chase, and exfiltrated on one shoe. The left heel and its wire crossed the border in a diplomatic pouch. The son kept the right one, we are told, on his desk, for years. The Ministry examined it seventeen times. There was never anything in it. That, MARRAINE said, was the design: give them a slipper to study, and they will never look for the foot.",)
