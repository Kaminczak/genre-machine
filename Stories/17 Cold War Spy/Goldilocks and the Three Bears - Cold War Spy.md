# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Cold War Spy

**Logline:** Agent Goldenrod breaches the Ursine safehouse in East Berlin. Three espressos cooling, three reading chairs, three trench coats. The cell returns from a dead drop.

## Script

("The safehouse on Bärenstrasse was rated three-key: three locks, three exits, three officers in residence, the Ursine cell, our best in the East. Agent GOLDENROD went in at 14:00 against orders, which the tribunal will note was her signature move. Kitchen: three espressos, still warm, which should have stopped her cold — you do not leave coffee in this business, you drink it or you are dead. The first cup was scalding: poured seconds before a rapid exit. The second iced: poured hours ago for show. The third was drinking temperature, and she drank it, which is how the Ursine cell learned everything they needed about her training. Three reading chairs, one with springs replaced by a pressure plate she triggered and defused with a hairpin, ruining it for future guests. Three cots in the back. She was found asleep on the smallest at 15:10 when the cell returned from a dead drop, and what saved her was the youngest officer's report, filed verbatim: subject asleep, one hand on sidearm, other hand on my chessboard, having improved my position against his father. She was traded west that winter for two of theirs. The Ursines still play the position. They call it the Goldenrod Defense.",)
