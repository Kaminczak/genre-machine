# Little Red Riding Hood - Cold War Spy

**Logline:** Operative Red crosses a Berlin checkpoint in a crimson coat with cakes-shaped microfilm. A wolf-handler wears grandmother's coat.

## Script

The checkpoint light was the color of weak tea, and Operative Red crossed under it with a basket of cakes for an ailing grandmother, which was true the way cover stories are true. Under the pastry: microfilm, sixty exposures, the entire Wolfhound network. Grandmother — codename for the sector's oldest handler, though she was also, inconveniently, an actual grandmother — kept a flat above the watchmaker's. The stairwell smelled wrong: someone else's cigarettes. The woman in the bed wore grandmother's coat with the collar up, and the collar was the tell; grandmother never wore it up, that was the duress signal, and this woman did not know it. What sharp eyes you have, Red said in the passphrase cadence. All the better, the woman answered, in the counter-phrase from the burned codebook, the one they had retired in March, because the wolves had it. Red set the basket down and left through the watchmaker's, as trained. The film went east in a coffin of cuckoo clocks. Grandmother was already in Vienna. The wolves got the cakes.
