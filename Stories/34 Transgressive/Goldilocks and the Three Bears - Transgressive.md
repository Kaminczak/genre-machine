# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Transgressive

**Logline:** Goldilocks breaks in because locks are a polite fiction. The porridge is property, property is theft, and she is hungry either way.

## Script

('Locks are a suggestion the poor make to each other so the rich can sleep. I want that understood before you hear what I did, because you already heard what I did: ate their food, broke their furniture, slept in their sheets, the whole nursery indictment. Here is what the story leaves out. That house had three of everything and the family was three; do the arithmetic on what I had. The county had moved me four times that year. Everything I owned answered to the word backpack. And their door — their door was not even locked, because people with three of everything believe the world is polite, and the world is polite, to them. The porridge was the first hot food in two days, and I ate the small one, note that, the small one, some vestigial manners operating even mid-crime. The chair broke because furniture built for people who have never missed a meal is not load-rated for the rest of us and that is a metaphor I did not ask to sit in. The bed, fine, the bed was greed, the bed was wanting one hour of what the boy had every night of his life: sleep with the door answered for. They caught me. The famous ending. What the story calls running away, I call the fourth eviction of the year. What it never asks: why a house with a spare of everything needed me gone. I did the dishes first. Nobody keeps that part.',)
