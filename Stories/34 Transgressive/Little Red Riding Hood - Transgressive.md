# Little Red Riding Hood - Transgressive

**Logline:** Red delivers what she delivers and doesn't ask questions. The wolf in grandmother's bed is the least dishonest thing in the family.

## Script

Deliveries are deliveries. You do not ask what is in the basket; the basket is a job, and the job pays for the apartment where nobody yells, which is all I ever wanted from any of them. My mother packs it. My mother has never once told me the truth about what my grandmother does, or why the men in this family leave, or what the red coat means — just wear it, just walk, just smile at the checkpoints of other people's curiosity. So when I found him in her bed, wearing her cardigan, doing his best impression of the woman none of us will discuss honestly, I did not scream. I sat down. I asked my questions to the only thing in that house that had ever bothered to disguise itself for me — that had done the reading, that knew her voice, her spectacles, her side of the bed. What big eyes you have, I said. Better to see you, he said, and he was the first one who ever had. The woodsman broke the door in later, all rescue, all noise. Everyone calls it the day I was saved. I keep the tooth in my coat pocket. Some honesty you have to bury to keep.
