# Three Little Pigs - Transgressive

**Logline:** Three squatter pig brothers refuse the mortgage, the landlord, and the wolf. The narrator is one of them, and he is not sorry.

## Script

The first rule of the straw house is that nobody owns the straw house. That was the whole sermon. My brother built with straw because straw is free and property is a con, and when the wolf came — and the wolf always comes, wearing a suit, carrying paperwork — he said the word eviction, and he huffed, and the straw went. My brother laughed while it went, which is the most honest laugh there is. The stick house lasted one day longer, which is exactly what compromise buys you. One day. Me, I built with brick. I know. I know. You spend your whole life spitting at the system and then you catch yourself mixing mortar at dawn, checking the level twice, liking it. He came down my chimney on a Tuesday, the suit, the teeth, the whole apparatus. There was a pot waiting. I want to tell you I planned it. I want to tell you a lot of things.
