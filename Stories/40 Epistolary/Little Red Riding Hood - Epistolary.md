# Little Red Riding Hood - Epistolary

**Logline:** Told in letters between Red and grandmother — and the increasingly suspicious postscripts written in a different hand.

## Script

Letter one, from R. to Grandmother: the path was lovely, I picked the flowers you like, expect me Thursday with cakes. Letter two, from Grandmother: come the long way, child, the woods are unquiet; also bring the wine. Letter three, from R.: who is this? Grandmother says the woods are lovely and to take the shortcut. The postscript is not in your hand. Letter four, from Grandmother, hurried: I wrote no such letter. Letter five, purporting to be from Grandmother, in handwriting that improves alarmingly with practice: disregard previous, am quite well, teeth much better, come at dusk, come alone. Letter six, from the Woodsman to R., enclosing letter five: found this nailed to a tree, thought you should see it, will walk past the cottage at four. Letter seven, from R. to the Wolf, addressed simply TO THE CURRENT OCCUPANT: sir, your postscripts gave you away in letter three — my grandmother never once, in sixty years of correspondence, ended a letter without asking whether I was eating enough. We will be there at four. We are bringing the axe and the wine. Letter eight, unsent, found later in the cottage, in the improving hand: to whom it may concern. The girl reads carefully. Nobody warns you about the ones who read carefully.
