# Cinderella - Epistolary

**Logline:** Assembled from the palace guest list, the glazier's invoice for a single slipper, and the stepmother's furious letters to an advice column.

## Script

Item 1. Invitation, gilt-edged, addressed to the Ladies of the Endry Household. Beneath, in pencil, in a young hand: all the ladies. it says all. Item 2. Note, the stepmother to the housekeeper: C. is not to be admitted to the fittings. She has duties. Item 3. Receipt, Mme. Ovett's Dressmaking, two gowns, RUSH. Item 4. Letter, unsent, C. to her late father: Papa. I am going anyway. I have found Mama's trunk. I hope that is allowed. I think it is allowed. Item 5. Statement, taken later, of the coachman-for-hire at the corner of Wren Lane: an old woman engaged me for the night, paid in old coin, and gave the address as the kitchen gate. I remember the coin. I remember the pumpkins on the gateposts. I remember thinking someone in that house was loved, whatever the front rooms thought. Item 6. The Palace Herald, morning edition: UNKNOWN LADY DEPARTS BALL AT MIDNIGHT; PRINCE RETAINS SLIPPER; INQUIRIES INVITED. Item 7. Letter, the stepmother to the Palace Chamberlain: re your inquiry, no member of this household attended in glass slippers. We are a house in half-mourning. We retire early. Item 8. Letter, the Palace Chamberlain to the stepmother: re your assurance, the Herald had not, in fact, mentioned glass. Item 9. Memorandum, palace counsel: the fitting tour may proceed, but counsel repeats its advice that the gentleman simply ask the lady's name at the next opportunity, counsel having reviewed the costs of the alternative. Item 10. Note, pinned to the kitchen gate, unsigned: he is coming up the lane with it. i have unlocked the gate. wear your mother's other one. — M. Ovett, who altered both gowns in that house for thirty years, and made no rush charge on the trunk work, and says nothing to anyone, and sees everything. Item 11. The Herald, special edition: FIT CONFIRMED AT KITCHEN GATE. Item 12. Invoice, Mme. Ovett's Dressmaking, one wedding gown, marked: no charge. paid in full the night she went anyway. Item 13. Placard, palace museum, east wing: THE SLIPPERS, GLASS, A PAIR. GIFT OF THE QUEEN, WHO REQUESTS THE CARD STATE PLAINLY THAT THEY WERE HER MOTHER'S, THAT THE TRUNK WAS IN THE ATTIC ALL ALONG, AND THAT THE ONLY MAGIC ANYONE HAS EVER PROVED IS AN OLD WOMAN WITH A COACH, A SEAMSTRESS WHO KEPT A KEY, AND A GATE SOMEONE LEFT UNLOCKED.
