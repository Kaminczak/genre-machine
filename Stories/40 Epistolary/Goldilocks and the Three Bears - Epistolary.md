# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Epistolary

**Logline:** Reconstructed from the bears' incident report, Goldilocks's unsent apology note, and a chair-repair invoice stamped DISPUTED.

## Script

Incident report, filed by B. Ursine Sr.: at approx. 3 PM, family returned from constitutional to find breakfast disturbed, one chair destroyed, one intruder asleep on premises. Intruder fled via window. No items taken except (see attached inventory) one (1) bowl porridge. — Attached, note in a child's hand: she also took my spoon. i want it back. it is the bear spoon. — Letter, one week later, no return address: Dear Ursine family, you do not know me but I was in your house. I am sorry about the chair. I am building you a new one in shop class. It is taking longer than expected because Mr. Feld says my joints are wrong. Enclosed is a spoon. I am sorry it is a different spoon. Yours sincerely, G. — Invoice, Feld's Woodshop, one chair (juvenile), marked DISPUTED, then marked PAID IN LABOR, then marked, in a third hand, PAID IN FULL AND THEN SOME, TELL HER SUPPER IS SUNDAYS. — Letter, four years later: Dear Ursines, they are moving me again. This county is the last one that has to take me. I measured the chair before I left. It held. — Note, pinned to a door, undated: G. The door is unlocked. It has always been unlocked. There are four bowls now. Stop knocking. — Museum placard, sixty years on: EXHIBIT 12. CORRESPONDENCE, URSINE FAMILY. ON LOAN FROM MRS. G. URSINE, WHO REQUESTS WE NOTE THE SPOON WAS RETURNED.
