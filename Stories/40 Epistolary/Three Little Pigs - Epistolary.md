# Three Little Pigs - Epistolary

**Logline:** Told in permits, insurance claims, and noise complaints: the wolf's letters grow unhinged as the brick house declines to answer.

## Script

Document one: a building permit for a dwelling of straw, approved by an inspector who noted, in pencil, his funeral. Document two: a letter from Mr. W. Wolf, concerned neighbor, to the planning board, regarding drafts. Document three: the board's reply, enclosing a pamphlet on breathing exercises. Document four: an insurance claim, cause of loss recorded as weather, allegedly. Document five: a second claim, identical, for the property next door. Document six: a noise complaint against the brick house at the end of the lane, citing smug masonry. Documents seven through eleven: letters from Mr. Wolf to the brick house itself, unanswered, increasingly personal in tone. Document twelve, recovered from the flue, soot-stained, unsigned: To whom it may concern. I am writing this from your roof. I have given the matter a great deal of thought, and I am coming down to discuss it in person. Document thirteen: a recipe for soup, annotated in three different hands, marked serves three. Generously.
