# Little Red Riding Hood - True Crime Doc

**Logline:** Re-enactment grain: girl in red last seen on woodland CCTV. The wolf left a tooth in the doorframe. Grandmother's body was never recovered.

## Script

The last confirmed image of her is a gas station camera on Route 9: a seventeen-year-old in a red windbreaker, buying two coffees at 4:12 PM. She told the clerk one was for her grandmother. The cottage sits eleven minutes up the fire road. Her car was found at the trailhead with the doors locked, basket gone. Investigators noted the cottage door showed no forced entry — the caller had been let in, or had a key, or had something better than either. On the doorframe, at shoulder height, a single tooth, canine, non-human, which the state lab logged and then lost, and then found, and then declined to discuss. Grandmother's body was never recovered. The bed had been slept in, the interviews say, by someone. Neighbors describe a man seen walking the fire road that week: tall, gray coat, a smile they all mention and none can describe. The case remains open. The girl in the red windbreaker remains missing. The tooth, wherever it is now, remains a tooth.
