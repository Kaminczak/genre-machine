# Cinderella - True Crime Doc

**Logline:** Camcorder reenactment: the stepmother, the stepsisters, the disappearance after the gala. The glass slipper, in evidence-bag, returns again to the cold case board.

## Script

("The last confirmed sighting of the girl the staff called Cindy is the Grandview Hotel ballroom's security camera, 11:57 PM, New Year's Eve, 1988: a young woman in a pale gown, running against the crowd, one shoe in her hand. At 11:59 she exits the frame. She does not appear on the lobby camera. She does not appear on the entrance camera. She does not appear again. What she left is the case's defining object: a glass slipper — custom, hand-ground, jeweler's work, the kind of thing that has a maker, except no maker was ever found. The stepfamily gave three interviews and three timelines. The stepmother said the girl had run off before; the sisters said she had never been at the ball; the guest list agreed, and the footage disagreed, and the footage was ruled inconclusive because the gown obscured her face. The prince — the hotel heir, who told police he had danced with her all evening and knew her laugh but not her name — funded the search for a decade: every shoemaker, every glazier, every size-six foot in three counties. The case went cold. The heir never married. And here the documentary lingers on its final exhibit, entered into evidence in 2003 when the stepmother's estate was probated: a second slipper, the mate, wrapped in a kitchen apron, in a locked hope chest she had told police was empty. The sisters declined comment. The heir did not. It is his only line on camera, and the film ends on it: she made it home, then. She made it home, and they knew.",)
