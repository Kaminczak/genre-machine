# Three Little Pigs - True Crime Doc

**Logline:** Camcorder talking heads recall the three Pig brothers, last seen alive. Detective Wolfe, retired, refuses to speak on camera.

## Script

The footage is from a camcorder, 1994. Three brothers, laughing, breaking ground on three properties off County Road 12. The youngest built fast and cheap; permits show the structure was up in nine days. The middle brother took a season. The eldest took two years and used masonry, and in the interviews you can hear the family still making jokes about it. On the night of October 14th the first property was reduced to debris. The sheriff's report calls it wind. On the 15th, the second. The report calls it wind again. Both brothers were then confirmed inside the third property, the brick one, where the damage stops. Detective Wolfe, retired now, worked the case for nine years and has declined every request for an interview, including ours. What is documented is this: something entered through the chimney. Something did not come back out. And all three brothers were alive at dawn.
