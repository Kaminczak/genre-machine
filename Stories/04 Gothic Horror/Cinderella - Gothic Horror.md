# Cinderella - Gothic Horror

**Logline:** An orphan kept in a manor cellar is granted one night of liberty by a veiled benefactor. A glass slipper is left on the stairs at twelve.

## Script

("The cellar of Aschenholm was dry, the housekeeper insisted, though the girl who slept there could hear water moving under the flags, patient as inheritance. Her stepmother kept the house in mourning that outlived its dead: crepe on the mirrors, her father's portrait turned to the wall, and the girl herself kept below stairs, ash-marked, half-servant, half-secret. The invitation to the ball came edged in black, as everything did that year. It was her mother's dress she wore in the end — found in the cellar, folded in cedar, fitting as if grief had kept her measurements — and her mother's slippers of glass, which the family Bible called by an older word: scrying-shoes. At the ball the prince danced only with her, and the candles guttered in a wind no one else remarked. At midnight the mirrors in the great hall gave up their crepe all at once. She fled, and the slipper she left behind showed the prince, when he lifted it, a cellar, a hearth, and water moving under stone. He came for her with the slipper in one hand and a lamp in the other. The stepmother met him at the door and said there was no one below. The house itself, they say, opened the cellar stair for him. Aschenholm approves of very few people.
