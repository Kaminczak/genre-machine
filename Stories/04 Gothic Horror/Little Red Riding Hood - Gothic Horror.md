# Little Red Riding Hood - Gothic Horror

**Logline:** A pale girl in scarlet velvet crosses moors to a fog-bound manor. The wolf has been the family curse for generations.

## Script

The moor fog took the village at dusk, as it did every dusk, and through it walked a pale girl in a scarlet cloak, the one drop of blood in all that gray. The manor at the wood's edge had been her family's for nine generations, and for nine generations something had scratched at its doors after midnight. Grandmother would not leave. The bloodline stays, she said, or the bargain breaks. Red carried candles and cake down the yew avenue while the portraits in her mind whispered their warnings. The great door stood open. In grandmother's chamber the fire had gone to embers, and the shape beneath the counterpane was too long, and the family curse looked out through borrowed eyes and said her name in a voice like a crypt door. What big eyes you have, said Red, drawing the old iron knife from the basket, beneath the cakes, where the women of her line had always carried it. The better, said the curse, and got no further.
