# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Gothic Horror

**Logline:** A blonde governess explores the locked east wing of a country estate. Three portraits, three chairs, three cold rooms. The Ursine family does not receive visitors.

## Script

("Thornfield Ursine had stood empty of guests for thirty years when the new governess arrived, golden-haired and unafraid, which the housekeeper noted with something like pity. The east wing is not opened, she was told. The family does not receive. But governesses walk at night; it is in the constitution of them. The east wing door was unlocked. Inside: three portraits with eyes that followed politely, three chairs arranged before a fire that had no business burning, three bowls set out on a table laid for no one. The first was scalding. The second ice. The third — warm, and fresh, and that was when she should have run. She sat in the smallest chair; it cracked like a pistol shot. Upstairs, three beds, and the smallest one aired and turned down as if expected. She woke at midnight to three figures at the bedfoot, great and middle and small, in mourning dress of a cut gone forty years. Someone has been sleeping in my bed, said the smallest, in a child's voice full of dust. And someone shall stay, said the largest, and the door closed itself, gently, the way well-bred houses do.",)
