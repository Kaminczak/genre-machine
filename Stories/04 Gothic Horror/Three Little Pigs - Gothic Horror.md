# Three Little Pigs - Gothic Horror

**Logline:** Three pig heirs inherit crumbling manors. A wolf-shaped family curse scratches at locked doors after midnight.

## Script

In the shadowed corners of the ancient village of Ravensbrook, three pig brothers sought to escape their family's cursed legacy. The youngest, Elric, moved into an old, cobweb-laden cottage made of rotting straw. The middle brother, Lysander, chose a dilapidated manor overgrown with thorny vines. The eldest, Moros, decided on a grand, looming castle made of dark, weathered bricks, rumored to be haunted. One fateful night, a mysterious and sinister wolf named Vlad emerged from the village's misty woods. Drawn to the cursed legacy, he easily entered Elric's cottage, pushing him to seek refuge in Lysander's vine-covered manor. But Vlad's dark magic tore through the manor, forcing both brothers to flee to Moros' haunted castle. As Vlad attempted to breach the castle's ancient barriers, ghostly apparitions, summoned by Moros, rose to defend their domain. Faced with supernatural protectors, Vlad vanished into the mist, leaving the brothers to dwell in the eerie safety of the haunted castle.
