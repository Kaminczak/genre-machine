# Three Little Pigs - Black Comedy

**Logline:** Three houses collapse; the paperwork is worse. A claims-adjusting wolf and the surviving pig work out who is liable for the chimney.

## Script

The first house fell on a Tuesday, which was awkward, because the memorial for the second house was already booked for Wednesday, and the caterer did not do refunds. The wolf, to his credit, filed everything. Act of breath, the claim said, which the insurer rejected, respiration being specifically excluded under section nine along with floods, wars, and grandmothers. The first two pigs moved in with the third, which the third described, in writing, as temporary. The wolf appealed. The appeal was denied. The wolf appealed the denial, and somewhere in a regional office a man who had not seen sunlight in eleven years stamped a form that made it all, technically, weather. Then the third pig read her policy — actually read it, cover to cover, a thing no one in the history of policies has done — and found the clause. Chimneys: homeowner's responsibility. So when the wolf came down hers on the last night of the fiscal year, he was, in the strictest liability sense, hers. The pot was insured. The wolf was not. The claim went through.
