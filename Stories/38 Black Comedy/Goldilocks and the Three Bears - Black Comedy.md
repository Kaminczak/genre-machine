# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Black Comedy

**Logline:** Goldilocks's estate sues the bears; the bears countersue over the chair. Everyone settles except the porridge.

## Script

("The Ursine family's suit was filed on a Monday: trespass, conversion of porridge, destruction of one (1) juvenile chair, emotional damages to a minor bear. Goldilocks's estate — she had, awkwardly, become a minor celebrity and then a major one and then dead, in the usual order — countersued by Thursday: attractive nuisance, unsecured premises, and a claim the tabloids fell on like porridge, defamation by nursery rhyme. Depositions were a carnival. Papa Bear, under oath, conceded the door was unlocked and had, quote, always been unlocked, we are bears, who robs bears. Mama Bear produced a receipt for the chair; the defense produced the chair, extant, repaired, and appraising at forty times the receipt as a cultural artifact, which the judge noted was the first time destruction of property had increased its value, and asked, wearily, if the porridge had appreciated too. The smallest plaintiff, now middle-aged, testified for eleven minutes and sank both cases: he had liked her, he said. He had liked having someone his size in the house, even asleep, even briefly. He had kept the chair broken for a year in case she came back to finish it. Everyone settled that afternoon. The porridge claim alone survives — neither side will drop it, on principle — and it is expected to outlive the court, the parties, and the concept of breakfast.",)
