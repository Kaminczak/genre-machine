# Little Red Riding Hood - Black Comedy

**Logline:** The wolf eats grandmother and inherits her book club, her feuds, and her cholesterol. Red arrives mid-meeting.

## Script

The wolf ate grandmother on a Tuesday and by Thursday deeply regretted it, because it turns out you inherit what you eat: the book club presidency, the feud with Doris, the standing hair appointment, and the cholesterol, which grandmother had cultivated for decades like a garden. The book club arrived at four. The wolf, in cap and gown, attempted the voice. Nobody noticed anything, which says more about book clubs than wolves. They were doing a novel about grief; the wolf had opinions; the opinions were well received, except by Doris, and thus the feud transferred cleanly to new management. Red arrived mid-meeting with the cakes and read the room in one professional glance: her grandmother was a wardrobe-thumping sound offstage, the wolf was chairing the discussion, and Doris was winding up. She freed grandmother, obviously — but not until the vote on next month's book, because grandmother, listening from the wardrobe, had shouted her proxy through the door. The wolf was made to resign the presidency, keep the cholesterol, and host in perpetuity. Doris got the feud back. The club agreed it was their best meeting in years, and the novel, everyone felt, had really understood loss.
