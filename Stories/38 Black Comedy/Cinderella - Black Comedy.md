# Cinderella - Black Comedy

**Logline:** The slipper fits eleven women and one duke. The prince's committee handles it badly. The pigeons handle the stepsisters, and the palace handles the press.

## Script

("The slipper fit eleven women and one duke, which is where the trouble started, though not — the palace press office would like this noted — where it ended. The Search Protocol had been announced in good faith: the prince would try the glass slipper on every eligible foot in the kingdom, identity to be established by fit. Nobody in the excitement consulted a cobbler. A cobbler would have mentioned that size six is the national median. The tour's first week produced four matches, two engagements-in-error, one lawsuit, and the Duke of Farrow, who had excellent bones, wore it better than anyone, and declined to take it off for three news cycles. The stepsisters, meanwhile, had pursued the traditional remedies — the elder had her toes done, the younger her heel, at the same discreet clinic, which billed the stepmother twice and, when she disputed the invoice, released the records to the press under the kingdom's new transparency laws, a scandal the papers ran under SISTERS SHAVE POINTS, PALACE SUSPECTS FOOT FRAUD. The prince, eleven fittings deep and engaged twice by accident, was on the verge of the committee's fallback proposal — a written exam about the evening, which the committee felt no impostor could pass and the prince felt he himself could not pass, having spent the ball, he admitted, looking mostly at her and not at, quote, the decorative particulars — when the girl herself ended it. She walked to the palace on a Tuesday, past the queue of claimants icing their feet, carrying the other slipper in a bread bag. The committee, presented with the pair, ruled the match inconclusive pending further fittings, at which point the prince, in the tour's only recorded act of executive decision, dismissed the committee, married the girl, and issued the one-sentence statement the palace still prints on commemorative tea towels: IT WAS NEVER ABOUT THE SHOE. The Duke of Farrow kept the original fitting-copy, wears it to the opening of Parliament annually, and has never once been asked to explain, which he reports is the whole pleasure. The clinic was acquitted. The stepsisters' suit against the clinic was not. The pigeons — asked about, in the old tellings, for what they did to the sisters' eyes — were, the record shows, never involved. The sisters did that to each other, at the wedding, over the bouquet. Some traditions survive every translation.",)
