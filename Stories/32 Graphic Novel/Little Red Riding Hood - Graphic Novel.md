# Little Red Riding Hood - Graphic Novel

**Logline:** Full-bleed panels of black woods and one screaming red hood. The wolf arrives in a single wordless spread.

## Script

PAGE ONE. Full bleed: black woods, vertical trunks like prison bars, and one small figure in a red hood, dead center, walking away from us. No caption. PAGE TWO, panel one: her boots on the path. Panel two: a second set of prints joining the path from the trees, larger, wrong. Panel three: just the basket, swinging. PAGE THREE: the cottage door, open exactly one wolf's-width. PAGE FOUR, splash: the bed, the shape in it, grandmother's cap over ears that will not stay inside the silhouette, and in the foreground, huge, Red's eye reflecting all of it in a single drop of light. PAGE FIVE, nine panels, silent: eyes. ears. teeth. Her hand. The basket. The wardrobe behind her, its door ajar, and inside it two eyes that are the right size. PAGE SIX, full bleed: red. Just red. PAGE SEVEN: the woods again, wide, the cottage small, smoke rising, and two figures in the doorway — one in a nightcap, one in a hood — and between the trees, walking away from us forever, a shape the caption finally names: SOME STORIES ONLY LOOK QUIET.
