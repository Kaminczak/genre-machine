# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Graphic Novel

**Logline:** Three-tier panels, one bowl per frame. The last page is a splash: three bears in the doorway, backlit.

## Script

("PAGE ONE, three tiers. Tier one: a forest road, tiny figure walking, gold scribble of hair. Tier two: a house among trees, door ajar, black interior. Tier three: just the door, closer, the black wider. PAGE TWO: the table, overhead angle, three bowls drawn in three line-weights — heavy, medium, fine. Panels of her hands: spoon in bowl one, recoil lines. Spoon in bowl two, frost hatching. Bowl three: no effect lines at all. Clean. Empty panel. Empty bowl. PAGE THREE: the chairs in profile, and the small one's panel border itself cracks when she sits, the frame splintering into the gutter. PAGE FOUR: stairs drawn as a vertical strip up the page's edge; her figure smaller in each step-panel; the bed at the top like a period at the end of a sentence. PAGE FIVE: black page. Single small caption, white: someone has been. PAGE SIX, splash: the doorway from inside the bedroom, three silhouettes filling it floor to lintel, backlit gold, her eye huge in the foreground panel-in-panel. No dialogue. PAGE SEVEN, three tiers again, mirroring page one: the window open, curtain blown. The road, tiny figure running, hair a gold scribble. The house among trees. In the last panel, smallest of the book: the small bear, righting his chair. Caption: and someone will be back.",)
