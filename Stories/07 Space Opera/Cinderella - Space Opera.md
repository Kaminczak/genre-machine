# Cinderella - Space Opera

**Logline:** A starship engine-room scrubber is given a princess-class kit by a rogue AI godmother. Her crystal envirosuit boot is left aboard the prince's flagship.

## Script

("Engine-room log, deep-hauler Cendrillon, entry 4,401: scrubbed the intake manifolds again, stepmother-captain logged my rations against it again, her daughters took my sleep shift again. Entry 4,402: the Fleet Ball is announced, orbit of the flagship, all crews invited. All crews but singular exceptions were listed. I am a singular exception. Entry 4,403 is not mine to write; the ship's derelict AI wrote it. It woke in the aft core where I eat my meals, called itself my godmother — an heirloom personality, my actual mother's service AI, presumed wiped. It had been hiding in the pumpkin — our slang for the emergency ascent pod, orange and round and officially inert. It printed me a gown out of solar-sail silk. It made the pod flightworthy with the mice — the six repair drones I fixed on my own shifts. It cut me a boarding pass the flagship believed. One constraint, it said: the pod's cell holds charge till 0000, then we fall out of the sky politely. The prince-admiral danced with the one guest who asked about his engine decks. At 2358 I ran. The boarding ramp took one boot — crystal envirosuit boot, my mother's, the pair of which I still wear. Entry 4,410: a fleet-wide query is asking every crew for the foot that fits a crystal boot. The stepmother-captain has confined me to the engine room. Entry 4,411: he searched the engine rooms first. He said afterward: obviously the engine rooms first. Where else would she be.",)
