# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Space Opera

**Logline:** A golden-haired stowaway boards an abandoned starship. Three engine cores, three command chairs, three cryo-pods. The Ursine crew returns from EVA.

## Script

("Stowaway's log. The ship read derelict from the pod: no hails, no running lights, hull cold. I docked because oxygen does not negotiate. Inside, artificial gravity and three of everything — three reactor cores in bay one, two spun down, one warm. Three command chairs on the bridge, and I sat in each: the captain's too large, its restraints swallowing me; the second rigged for someone half my mass; the third fit like it was printed for me, and I logged that without asking myself the obvious question. Three cryo-pods aft, two sealed and frosted, one open and freshly cycled. I was tired in the way that gets stowaways killed. I climbed into the open pod, just to warm up, just for a minute. I woke to three faces in the viewport of the pod — vacuum-suited, broad, unmistakably ursine, home from EVA with hull plating under their arms. Someone has been in my reactor, said the largest over comms. Someone has been in my chair. The smallest one just looked at me through the glass of its own pod, which I was in. They spaced my excuses, kept me for the work, and I have been crew ever since. The fourth chair got printed last month.",)
