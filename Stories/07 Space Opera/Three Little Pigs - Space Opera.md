# Three Little Pigs - Space Opera

**Logline:** Three pig colonists raise domes on a frontier moon. A wolf-class warship in low orbit picks them off, dome by dome.

## Script

On the frontier moon of Kepler's Trough, three colonist siblings raised three domes. Tam's was polymer sheeting, up in a day, glorious in the low light. Corin's was sintered regolith, honest work, six weeks of it. Vesper's was fused ceramic plate, twelve months of labor, and the colony called her paranoid to her face. Then a wolf-class raider slipped into low orbit with no transponder and no demands. It vented Tam's dome in one pass; she made the crawler tunnel with sixteen seconds of air. It cracked Corin's on the second. Both of them huddled in Vesper's ceramic shell while the raider burned its entire magazine against plate rated for meteor strike. Out of ordnance, it tried the atmospheric intake. Vesper had rigged the intake to the reactor purge. The colony renamed the dome Patience, and never called her paranoid again.
