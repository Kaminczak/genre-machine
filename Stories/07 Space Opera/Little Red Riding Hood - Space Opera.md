# Little Red Riding Hood - Space Opera

**Logline:** A cadet in a red flight jacket pilots between asteroids to grandmother's mining station. A wolf-pirate has docked first.

## Script

Cadet log, day forty: the run to grandmother's mining station threads the asteroid belt, and command says never leave the marked corridor. The med-supplies rode in a red-striped pod behind my seat. A freighter hailed me halfway out — friendly voice, asked my route, wished me clear skies. I logged it and forgot it. Docking bay nine was already cycled when I arrived, which was wrong; grandmother seals everything twice. The figure at the comm station wore her flight jacket with the patches peeled. What happened to your eyes, I said, and the pirate smiled with a mouthful of black-market chrome. Better cameras than the old lady's, kid. The station's better guns answered before I could: grandmother had never been in the comm chair. She was in the turret, where she has been every docking for thirty years. We ate the cakes watching his engine flare dwindle toward the belt. Never leave the corridor, she said. Unless you are the turret.
