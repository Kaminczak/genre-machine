# Little Red Riding Hood - Magical Realism

**Logline:** Grandmothers in the village turn into wolves at a certain age; it is considered rude to mention. Red brings cake anyway.

## Script

In the village it is known, in the way weather is known, that the grandmothers turn into wolves at a certain age. No one says so at table. One year the shawl, the next a certain length of tooth, and eventually the walk to the woods that is not discussed, after which the cottage stands empty and full at once. It is considered rude to mention, and ruder to grieve early. Red's grandmother was at the in-between age: hands still hands, but the door of her cottage now scratched on the inside, and the cakes Red carried had changed recipe, on her mother's instructions, to things a longer jaw could love. What big eyes you have, Red said each Sunday, because it was the courtesy, the way one says how young you look to the aging. The better to see you, dear, said grandmother, each Sunday, later and later from the throat. The Sunday the bed stood empty, Red walked out past the garden and left the basket at the treeline, and did not look up, because looking is mentioning. From the pines, a breathing. On the gate on her way out: one long gray hair, left the way flowers are left. She wove it into her hood. It is what you do. It is what everyone's hood is.
