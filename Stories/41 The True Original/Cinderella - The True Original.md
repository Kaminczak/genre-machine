# Cinderella - The True Original

**Logline:** A hazel tree, a wishing bird, stepsisters who cut off toe and heel, and doves that peck out their eyes. Grimm's Aschenputtel.

## Script

When the rich man's wife lay dying, she bade her only daughter be good and pious, and she would watch over her from heaven. A hazel twig grew into a tree on the mother's grave, watered by the girl's tears, and a little white bird upon it granted whatever she wished. But her father took a new wife, who brought two proud daughters, and they made the girl sleep in the ashes and called her Cinderella. When the King held a three-day feast, the bird clothed her in gold, and the prince danced with none but her, though each night she fled. On the palace stairs she left a golden slipper. The prince sought its owner house to house. The eldest stepsister cut off her toe to force the shoe on; the younger cut off her heel; but two white doves cried that blood was in the shoe, and the prince turned each false bride away. Cinderella's foot slipped in true. And at her wedding the doves flew down and pecked out both stepsisters' eyes, and so for their wickedness they were punished with blindness all their days.
