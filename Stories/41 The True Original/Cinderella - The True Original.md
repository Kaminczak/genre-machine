# Cinderella - The True Original

**Logline:** A hazel tree, a wishing bird, stepsisters who cut off toe and heel, and doves that peck out their eyes. Grimm's Aschenputtel.

## Script

The wife of a rich man fell sick, and called her only daughter to her bedside, and said, "Dear child, be good and pious, and then the good God will always protect thee, and I will look down on thee from heaven." A little hazel-tree grew upon the mother's grave, watered by the maiden's tears, and a white bird upon it granted whatever she wished. But her father took a new wife, who brought two proud daughters, and they made the maiden sleep among the ashes, and called her Cinderella. When the King's son gave a festival, the bird clothed her in gold, and the prince would dance with none but her, though each night she fled. On the stairs she left a slipper of gold. Then the eldest sister cut off her toe to force on the shoe, and the second cut off her heel; but two white doves cried, "Turn and peep, blood is in the shoe." So the prince carried each false bride back. And when Cinderella's foot fitted true, the doves flew down and pecked out one eye from each of the proud sisters; and so, for their wickedness and falsehood, they were punished with blindness all their days.
