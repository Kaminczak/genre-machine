# Little Red Riding Hood - The True Original

**Logline:** The wolf swallows grandmother and Red-Cap whole; a huntsman cuts them out alive and fills him with stones. Grimm, unsoftened.

## Script

Once there was a sweet little maid who wore a cap of red velvet, and so was called Little Red-Cap. Her mother sent her through the wood to her sick grandmother, with cake and wine, and bade her keep to the path. But a wolf met her, and learned where she was going. He ran ahead, swallowed the grandmother whole, put on her cap and gown, and lay down in her bed. When Red-Cap came, she marveled: what great ears you have, what great eyes, what great hands -- and what a dreadful great mouth! And the wolf sprang out of bed and swallowed her too. Then, being full, he slept and snored aloud. A huntsman passing by heard him, and thinking the old woman ill, went in. He took a pair of shears and cut open the sleeping wolf's belly, and out sprang Red-Cap, and then her grandmother, both alive. They filled the wolf's belly with heavy stones, and when he woke and tried to run, the stones dragged him down, and he fell dead.
