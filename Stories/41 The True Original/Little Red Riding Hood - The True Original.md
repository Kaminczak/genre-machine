# Little Red Riding Hood - The True Original

**Logline:** The wolf swallows grandmother and Red-Cap whole; a huntsman cuts them out alive and fills him with stones. Grimm, unsoftened.

## Script

Once upon a time there was a dear little maiden, and her grandmother gave her a little cap of red velvet, which suited her so well that she would never wear anything else; so she was called Little Red-Cap. Her mother sent her through the wood to her grandmother, who was ill, and bade her not leave the path. But the Wolf met her, and learned whither she was going. He ran on before, and swallowed up the grandmother, and put on her cap and gown, and lay down in her bed. "Oh, grandmother, what great ears you have!" "The better to hear thee with." "What great eyes you have!" "The better to see thee with." "What a horrible great mouth you have!" "The better to eat thee with!" And scarcely had the Wolf said this, than with one bound he sprang from the bed and swallowed up Red-Cap. Then, being full, he slept and snored aloud. A huntsman passing by cut open the sleeping Wolf, and out sprang Red-Cap, and then the old grandmother, both alive. They filled the Wolf's body with great heavy stones, and when he awoke and would have run, the stones dragged him down, and he fell dead.
