# Cinderella - High Fantasy

**Logline:** A scullery girl is gifted gown and crystal slippers by a fae godmother. At the royal court ball, the spell unravels at the witching hour.

## Script

("In the court of the Thornwake kings, the girl was called Cinderwene, daughter of a dead knight, ward of a living grievance. Her stepmother had rank-magic — the petty, legal kind, wards on doors and locks on futures — and had bound the girl to hearth-service with a contract signed in her grief-year, which is when the law lets you sign anything. The Midsummer Ball was contractually beyond her. But her mother's people had been forest, not court, and on Midsummer the forest collects its debts: the fae godmother arrived not kindly but punctual, an aunt in the old sense, terrible and obliged. The gown she spun from moth-wing and law — for fae magic is all terms — and the slippers she blew from glass with these conditions: they fit only feet that have carried burdens unthanked; they cannot be sold, stolen, or inherited; they end at midnight, for even the fae respect the hour contracts turn. The prince knew rank-magic when he saw its absence: the one dancer at the ball wearing nothing borrowed against her future. At midnight the working closed and she fled it gracefully — the slippers, being terms, stayed behind of their own accord, one on the stair, one already walking to the prince's hand. The kingdom's mages tried it on every noble foot for a month, and it slid off ranked heels like water off oath-breakers. It fit the hearth-girl at Thornwake. It had been, the fae aunt observed at the wedding, in a tone that closed all appeals, a fitting from the start. The stepmother's contract was reviewed. Grief-year signatures, the new queen ruled, are void. All of them. Everywhere. The forest approved.",)
