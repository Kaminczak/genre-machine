# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - High Fantasy

**Logline:** A blonde apprentice trespasses in the cottage of three were-bear druids. Three goblets of mead, three oaken thrones, three furred sleeping mats.

## Script

("The apprentice had been warned about the grove: three thrones of living oak, three goblets that refill themselves, three sleeping mats of moss that dream. The were-bear druids of the Urswood suffer no trespass, said her master, so naturally she went in, golden braid and all, the day her provisions ran out. The first goblet burned like dragonfire mead. The second was meltwater cold, tasting of winters before men. The third was just right, honey and summer, and she drank it down — and felt the grove notice her, the way a sleeping thing notices a fly. She sat the smallest throne to steady herself and it cracked down its heartwood with a sound like an oath breaking. The moss took her before she could run; that is what the moss is for. She woke ringed by three shapes between bear and elder: the Great one smelling of storms, the Middle one crowned in ivy, the Small one — her own age, whatever age that was — looking at the broken throne he had grown from an acorn over ninety years. Someone has drunk the summer cup, said the Great. The price is a season's service. She served it. She is serving it still, some say, and the small throne is growing back crooked, and the grove has begun, quietly, to grow a fourth.",)
