# Little Red Riding Hood - High Fantasy

**Logline:** An apprentice mage in a red enchanted hood crosses an ensorcelled wood to grandmother, a retired battlemage. A werewolf knight bars the path.

## Script

The hood was her grandmother's last great working: crimson wool, ward-woven, proof against glamour, hex, and most kinds of lying. Red walked the ensorcelled wood where the path rearranged itself for travelers it disliked, carrying honeycakes and a replacement focus-crystal, for grandmother had burned hers out again dueling the frost that kept creeping down from the tors. At the ford a knight barred the way, armor black, visor down, and the hood grew hot as a stove lid against her shoulders. Stand aside, sir, said Red. The knight's helm came off, and the wolf beneath it spoke with a man's grief: I was cursed at this crossing, and I bar it to spare travelers the hour of my changing. The moon is rising. Run. Red did not run. She was her grandmother's blood, and the hood knew a curse when it warmed. She threw it over his shoulders as the moonlight struck. What walked on with her to grandmother's cottage was a man, weeping, carrying her basket, finally cold.
