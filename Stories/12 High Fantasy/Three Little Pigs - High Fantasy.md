# Three Little Pigs - High Fantasy

**Logline:** Three pig brothers each enchant a magical material — golden hay, runed oak, dwarven brick. A werewolf knight rides at the blood moon.

## Script

In the vale beneath the Thornwake, three pig brothers each bound a house to a working. Elowen enchanted golden hay, harvest-blessed, warm as a granted wish and about as durable. Fenwick raised oak carved with binding runes, each one paid for in a year of his life. Gareth quarried dwarven brick from the deep road and set it with mortar sung over by the stone-mothers for nine nights. At the blood moon a werewolf knight rode down the vale with a lance of cold iron. Harvest-blessing is a summer magic; the hay went up like a promise. Fenwick's runes held for three passes and then unwrote themselves in the wrong order. Behind dwarven brick the brothers waited while iron rang on stone and stone answered nothing. The knight sought the smoke-hole, as the old songs warn against. Below him the stone-mothers' fire had been waiting nine nights to be useful.
