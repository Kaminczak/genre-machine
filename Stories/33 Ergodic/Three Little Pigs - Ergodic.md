# Three Little Pigs - Ergodic

**Logline:** A recovered case file about three houses. The footnotes contradict the appendix, the appendix contradicts the house, and the wolf is in the margins.

## Script

Exhibit B. Recovered from the site. Annotations in three hands, none identified. The straw house, see note seven, was a conceptual failure of structure, not material. Note seven: there was no straw house. The stick house appears only in the appendix, and the appendix has been redacted. Of the brick house we possess the blueprint, the invoice, and a floor plan that does not close — four walls on paper, five when walked. Marginal hand one writes: huff and puff are breathing exercises. Marginal hand two writes: the chimney is a lie. Marginal hand three, later, shakier: the chimney is a door. The wolf is not described anywhere in the file. The wolf is the file. Page seven is a maze drawn in pencil, three small figures at the center, and an eraser mark circling them, patiently, from the outside. They never left the labyrinth. You are holding it.
