# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Romance

**Logline:** A blonde botanist seeks shelter from a storm in a forest home. Three bear-shifters return — Papa, Mama, and the youngest, who cannot look away.

## Script

('The storm came off the ridge with no warning worth the name, and the botanist — golden hair plastered to her collar, press full of specimens getting ruined — took the only shelter the mountain offered: a timber house with woodsmoke rising and nobody home. She knocked, waited, and let the weather make the decision. Inside: three bowls of stew on the table, and she was too cold for manners — the first scalded, the second had gone stone cold, the third was perfect and she ate it shaking. She hung her coat, sat in the smallest chair by the fire — which cracked, because everything she touched today broke — and fell asleep in it anyway, mid-apology to no one. The Ursin family came home to find her there: father, mother, and the youngest, who was twenty-six that spring and stopped in the doorway as if the weather had followed him in. She woke mortified, offering money, offering to leave into the storm, and the youngest heard himself say the stew had needed eating and the chair had needed breaking, it was a terrible chair, he had made it himself and could he make her a better one. He could. It took him all winter, because she kept visiting the workshop, and the chair kept needing, somehow, another coat of finish. It sits by their own fire now. It is just right, and it took him years to stop apologizing for the first one.',)
