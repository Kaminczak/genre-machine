# Three Little Pigs - Romance

**Logline:** Three pig siblings in Heartsville each chase love their own way — letters, serenades, lavish balls. A heartbroken wolf named Lysander arrives and becomes their closest friend.

## Script

In the picturesque town of Heartsville, three pig siblings embarked on quests for true love. The youngest, Liora, lived in a quaint straw cottage, penning love letters to a distant suitor she had yet to meet. Eamon, the middle sibling, resided in a cozy stick bungalow, frequently serenading the object of his affections under moonlit nights. The eldest, Seraphina, maintained a grand brick mansion, hosting lavish balls in hopes of finding a partner who loved her for who she was, not her wealth. As fate would have it, a charming wolf named Lysander arrived in town, his heart having been broken before. Liora's letters touched his soul, Eamon's songs mended his spirit, and Seraphina's genuine kindness taught him to trust again. Instead of a foe, Lysander became their closest confidant. Through shared experiences, the four discovered that love, in all its forms, was the true treasure.
