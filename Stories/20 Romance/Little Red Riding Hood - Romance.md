# Little Red Riding Hood - Romance

**Logline:** A young woman in a red riding cloak is charmed by a stranger on the path. Grandmother, at the cottage, simply pours three cups.

## Script

The stranger fell into step with her where the path bent through the birches, and he had the kind of smile that made you check your basket was still on your arm. Bold of you, he said, red, in a wood like this. Bold of you, she said, talking to it. He laughed, and the laugh was better than the smile. He knew the names of the flowers she picked; he knew which bridge was rotten; he walked her the long way without once suggesting the short one. At grandmother's gate he stopped, suddenly awkward, a man out of moves. I should tell you, he said, people around here call me the wolf. I know, she said. Grandmother told me. She has been watching from the window since the birches. The cottage door opened on the smell of cakes, and grandmother stood there with three cups already poured, wearing the expression of a woman who has been right about something for weeks. Well, she said. Are you both going to stand there, or is he coming in?
