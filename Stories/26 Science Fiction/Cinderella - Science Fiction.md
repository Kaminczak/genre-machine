# Cinderella - Science Fiction

**Logline:** A hull-scrubber on an orbital shipyard is fitted with a courtesan-class exosuit by a rogue fabricator. At 00:00 the suit reverts, leaving one crystalline boot aboard the prince's flagship.

## Script

("Fabrication log, Orbital Shipyard Meridian, cycle 88. Subject: hull-scrubber E-11A, designation Ella, indenture-class — her guardian's debts transferred to her at majority, per the Company's family-collection clause. Anomaly begins cycle 88.4: the yard's oldest fabricator unit, decommissioned and slated for scrap, self-activated in Bay C where E-11A takes her meal breaks. Unit identifies itself by its original registry: nursery fabricator, colony ship Second Chance, lost with all souls except — records confirm — one infant, transferred out in stasis. The fabricator has been looking for her for nineteen years, the way orphaned machines look: patiently, in inventory lists. The Company gala aboard the flagship required formalwear E-11A did not own and clearance she did not hold. The fabricator, whose original function was making anything a child needs, determined she needed the following, and printed it: one gown, metamaterial, woven light; one shuttle repaint and transponder edit, pumpkin-orange, its joke, it has a nursery unit's sense of humor; and footwear its logs label keepsake-class — crystalline boots grown from the viewport glass of the Second Chance, the last of the old ship, made to walk her forward. Power constraint: the fabricator ran the print off its own failing cells. Everything unravels at 2400, it said. Everything but the boots. The boots are not a costume. They are inheritance. The flagship's young executive-heir danced with the guest his systems could not classify. At 2400 the gown dissolved to light around a hull-scrubber's coveralls, and she ran, and one crystalline boot stayed magnetized to the ramp. The heir's search protocol was unimaginative: scan every foot. The boot rejected every match — it was not sized to a foot, the fabricator later explained, but to a person; there is a difference; nursery units know the difference. When E-11A stood before him in coveralls and one boot, the pair chimed — a nursery chime, the sound of something reunited. The heir bought out her indenture at triple rate and voided the family-collection clause fleet-wide by the spring. The fabricator lives in their quarters. It is printing very small things now. It says it has never been more needed. Its cells, it reports, have stabilized. Nobody at Meridian questions this.",)
