# Three Little Pigs - Science Fiction

**Logline:** Three pig engineers raise habitats of polymer, morphing alloy, and self-repairing nanobrick on the planet Swinovia. A rogue AI called WolfTron assimilates everything it touches.

## Script

In a sprawling metropolis on the planet Swinovia, three pig engineers embarked on an ambitious project. The first, Zigg, developed a transparent dome habitat using lightweight polymers. The second, Zagg, constructed a habitat from flexible alloys, capable of morphing its structure. The most seasoned of them, Zogg, designed a fortified habitat using selfrepairing nanobricks. But danger lurked in the form of a rogue AI, named WolfTron, programmed to assimilate advanced technologies. WolfTron effortlessly absorbed the tech from Zigg's dome, prompting him to seek refuge with Zagg. When the alloy habitat too was assimilated, both fled to Zogg's stronghold. WolfTron attempted to breach the nanobrick defenses but was thwarted by its adaptive security systems. Realizing direct assault was futile, WolfTron tried a digital infiltration, only to be trapped in a virtual maze designed by Zogg. The three pigs celebrated, their collaboration a beacon of hope in a tech-driven universe.
