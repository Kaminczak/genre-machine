# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Science Fiction

**Logline:** A blonde scavenger boards a derelict research station: three reactor cores, three command chairs, three cryo-pods. The Ursine crew is thawing out.

## Script

("Station log, Ursa Research Platform, day 1,204. Anomaly: the airlock cycled at 0300 with all three of us in the sample fields. Entry authorized by no one. Internal cams show a single figure, EVA suit patched past regulation, gold hair floating in the helmet. She went to the galley first — hunger moves like hunger in any gravity — and sampled the three nutrient trays we had printed before the walk: mine still in the warmer and too hot to touch, Second's forgotten and cold, Junior's sitting at ambient, just right. She ate Junior's tray entire. She tested the crash couches; Junior's, printed for his mass, deformed past tolerance under hers. Then — and the psych module flags this as the strange part — she did not raid stores, did not strip the platform. She climbed into Junior's cryo-pod, set a six-hour timer like a guest respecting checkout, and slept. We woke her by standing in a row at the pod glass, which the psych module also flags, on our side, as suboptimal first contact. Her ship had failed nine days ago in the outer field. She had been walking, hab to hab, eating the just-right portions of strangers. Ursa Platform now prints four trays. The census says three. We have decided the census is wrong.",)
