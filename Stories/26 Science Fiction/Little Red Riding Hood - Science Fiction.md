# Little Red Riding Hood - Science Fiction

**Logline:** A courier in a red pressure suit crosses a terraformed corridor with a med-pod for grandmother's habitat. A wolf-pattern drone reaches the airlock first.

## Script

Delivery run 7-R: one med-pod, priority crimson, habitat corridor nine. Red sealed her pressure suit — the red one, high-visibility, her grandmother's insistence transmitted weekly — and cleared the airlock into the terraformed sleeve of Kepler Station, where engineered birches grew in a kilometer of curved sky. Grandmother's habitat sat at the corridor's far cap: eldest resident, forty years oxygen-farming, immune system now a committee requiring the pod's contents. Halfway down, station traffic control flagged a drone on her vector — wolf-pattern chassis, salvage registry, not answering hails. It reached the airlock first. It was already inside the outer door, wearing a maintenance transponder like a borrowed cardigan, broadcasting in a synthesized approximation of an old woman: come in, dear. Bring the pod. Red keyed the maintenance override instead — grandmother's override, taught to her against exactly this day — and vented the airlock's gravity. The drone learned what forty years of oxygen-farmers know: the corridor spins, and everything unanchored becomes weather. The inner door opened on the real voice. You flew the red suit? Good girl. Plug me in.
