# Three Little Pigs - Survival Wilderness

**Logline:** Three pig brothers stranded in a frozen tundra build shelters of caribou hide, driftwood, and packed snow. A timber wolf circles, patient as winter.

## Script

Forty below on the Kesnik flats, and three brothers with one night to get it right. Aldo stretched caribou hide over a frame and had shelter in an hour, which is fast, and warm, and thin. Bo lashed driftwood into a lean-to and packed the gaps with moss, four hours, better odds. Cal cut snow blocks and built a proper spiral dome, worked until his hands stopped reporting in, and finished after dark to no applause. The timber wolf came at moonrise, patient as winter itself. It went through the hide like a page. It worried the driftwood loose piece by piece until the lean-to gave up its heat. Then it circled the snow dome, which is the thing about snow: it is soft, and it does not break, it only presses back. Inside, three brothers and one small flame. Above them, the vent hole. Below the vent hole, a stove.
