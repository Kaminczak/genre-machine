# Three Little Pigs - Steampunk

**Logline:** Three pig engineers fortify workshops with steam and brass. The wolf arrives in an airship of pressurized fury.

## Script

In the soot-gold city of Cogsmere, three pig engineers filed three very different patents. Percival threw up a workshop of woven straw and steam-lacquer, cheap and quick and warm. Barnaby raised a lattice of oiled timber, riveted at every joint. Augustine, who read failure reports for pleasure, built in brick, and laced the brick with pressure plates and a boiler he named Clementine. Then came Lord Fenrick's airship, all brass ribs and pressurized fury, its bellows engine designed for precisely one purpose: to blow. Percival's lacquer went up in a breath. Barnaby's rivets sang and surrendered. Against Augustine's brick the airship spent its whole reservoir and achieved nothing but a very loud apology. Fenrick descended by the chimney, as gentlemen villains do, and met Clementine at full pressure. The lesson, entered into the record: redundancy is not cowardice. It is engineering.
