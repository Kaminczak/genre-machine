# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Steampunk

**Logline:** A golden-haired aerial scout breaks into a brass workshop — three boilers, three benches, three berths. Three mechanical bears clank up the path.

## Script

("The aerial scout put down in the Ursine workshop yard with a punctured envelope and no appointment. The doors stood open — trusting folk, engineers — and inside, three of everything, in ascending bore: three boilers ticking over, three benches with the tools laid true, three berths behind the flywheel wall. The scout, being golden-haired and low on options, sampled the boilers' cocoa spigots: the first ran scalding, the second cold as regret, the third just right. She borrowed the smallest bench to patch her envelope and broke its leg clean off — cheap casting, she noted professionally. Then, because altitude makes you honest about exhaustion, she took the smallest berth. She woke to three mechanical bears clanking up the path, brass heads swiveling, gauges going red in sequence. SOMEONE HAS OPERATED MY SPIGOT, boomed the largest in a voice like a train arriving. SOMEONE HAS STRESSED MY BENCH, tolled the second. The smallest just pointed one articulated claw at its berth, and its steam whistle went up an octave. The scout paid for the bench in valve work and left with an escort of three. Engineers, it turns out, forgive trespass. They invoice it.",)
