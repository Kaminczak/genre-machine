# Cinderella - Steampunk

**Logline:** A workshop drudge is fitted with brass corset and clockwork gown. Her glass-and-copper boot detaches when the pneumatic carriage runs out of pressure.

## Script

("The Aldermans' scullery ran on one overworked girl and a boiler she had privately named Hope, and both were due for a pressure failure the night of the Exposition Ball. Enter, through the coal chute, in defiance of several patents, her godmother — the family's disgraced aunt, an engineer struck from the Guild rolls for unlicensed wonders. From carpet bag: one corset of sprung brass, load-bearing; one gown of oil-silk that shed cinders like water; two slippers of annealed glass, heeled with rubber, rated to a running gait. From the kitchen garden: one pumpkin, which under the aunt's spanner became a carriage with a two-stroke heart and a temper. The terms were mechanical, not magical: the boiler I have borrowed against holds pressure until midnight, the aunt said, and after that, physics resumes. The prince — in truth the Exposition's chief judge, young, ink-stained, bored of gilding — danced with the one guest who could name every alloy in the chandeliers. At 11:58 she ran, threw the regulator, and made the gate as Hope sighed her last. One slipper, glass and rubber, remained on the steps. He tested its tolerances personally. Then he tested every foot in the city, which is not romance, he insisted, but proper experimental method. The fit, when found, was flush to the thousandth. They hold the patent jointly.",)
