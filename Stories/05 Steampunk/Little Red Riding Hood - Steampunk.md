# Little Red Riding Hood - Steampunk

**Logline:** A girl in a brass-buttoned red coat steams through clockwork woods on a velocipede. A wolf-engineer ambushes her near grandmother's pneumatic cottage.

## Script

Red's velocipede hissed steam at every stroke as she pedaled the clockwork wood, where brass birds ticked in the branches and the trees ran on mainsprings wound each spring by the Forestry Guild. In her basket: cakes, and a fresh pressure valve for grandmother's cottage, which ran on pneumatics and stubbornness. She found the door cycled open and the cottage exhaling steam it should have kept. At the bedside console sat a figure in grandmother's goggles, all wrong at the joints. What large optics you have, said Red, hand drifting to her wrench. The better to calibrate you, my dear, said the wolf-engineer, and lunged — into the path of the counterweight Red had already released. Gears sang, pistons answered, and the intruder left through the wall grandmother had always meant to renovate. Grandmother emerged from the boiler room, oil to the elbows. Good valve, she said, inspecting the basket. Better girl.
