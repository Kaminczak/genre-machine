# Cinderella - Film Noir

**Logline:** A jazz-club hatcheck girl is transformed for one night by a chain-smoking godmother. She loses a glass heel as the clock strikes twelve.

## Script

("She checked hats at the Pumpkin Club, and the ashes she came home to were the kind you find in ashtrays. The stepfamily ran the place: the widow at the books, the two daughters on the floor, and the kid doing every job neither glamour nor cruelty wanted. The night of the Marchetti gala, a woman I never made walked into my office smelling of woodsmoke and turned my client's hatcheck girl into a headline: gown, wheels, the works, borrowed against a deadline she was straight with me about. Midnight, she said. After that the gown's a pumpkin and the girl's a warrant. She danced with the prince of the city — the DA's untouchable son — and at 11:59 she ran like the truth was after her, which it was. All she left was a shoe. Glass. Size small, heart big, no prints. The kid himself came to me a week later with it wrapped in newspaper, wanting her found, and I did what you do with the only honest thing in a dirty town: I found the foot it fit. She was back at the hatcheck window. She had never left. In this town even miracles clock back in by morning.",)
