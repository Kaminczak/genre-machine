# Three Little Pigs - Film Noir

**Logline:** Three pig brothers run a smoky speakeasy. A wolf detective in a fedora circles, looking for the hideout no bullet can crack.

## Script

The city was three parts rain and one part bad idea. The Pig brothers ran the Straw Room, a speakeasy off Ninth, and business was the kind that gets people killed. Angelo bet on charm and cheap timber. Vincent bet on lumber and locks. Salvatore, the eldest, bet on brick, because brick does not argue. A wolf named Lucian Vell walked in on a Tuesday wearing a fedora and a badge he had not earned. He blew the doors off the Straw Room, and Angelo ran. He leaned on Vincent's place until the walls quit, and two brothers ran. Then he came to the brick. He huffed until his lungs turned to glass and the wall just stood there, patient as a tombstone. He went for the chimney, the way desperate men always go for the chimney. Salvatore had a pot on. In this town, the smart money keeps something boiling.
