# Little Red Riding Hood - Film Noir

**Logline:** A redhead in a crimson trench coat slips through fog to a tenement. A wolf in a gray suit is already inside, wearing grandmother's spectacles.

## Script

The rain had opinions that night, all of them bad. She came down Delancey in a crimson trench with a basket on her arm — cakes, she said, though nobody carries cakes at midnight. Grandmother's tenement, third floor, the door unlatched. That was wrong. Grandmother locked up like a bank. Inside, the lamp was low and the figure in the bed wore grandmother's shawl and grandmother's spectacles, and neither fit. Nice eyes you got, she said, testing. Better to see you, sweetheart. Big hands, too. Better to hold you. She set the basket down slow. Grandma, she said, never called anybody sweetheart in her life. The shawl came off, and the gray suit under it, and the teeth. What happened next the neighbors heard three ways. All three versions agree on the ending: a wolf on the fire escape, running, and a girl in red standing over an empty bed, already knowing the basket had been the point all along.
