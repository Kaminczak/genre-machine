# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Slipstream

**Logline:** The porridge is too hot, then too cold, then too Tuesday. The chairs remember being trees. Goldilocks has been asleep in the third bed for an hour, or a life.

## Script

('The porridge was too hot, then too cold, then too Tuesday, and Goldilocks had learned not to remark on the third category. The house belonged to bears the way Tuesdays belong to weeks: officially, and without enthusiasm. She ate what the bowls permitted. The chairs remembered being trees — the big one remembered a storm it had loved, the middle one remembered birds, and the small one remembered being a sapling so hard, so completely, that it went back to it under her, all splinters and green ambition, and she apologized to the wood, which is the correct thing and she knew it. Upstairs the beds were made in three tenses: the big one had been slept in, the middle one was being slept in — although it was empty, it was being slept in, this was evident and unexplainable — and the small one would be slept in, had always been going to be slept in, by her, since before houses. She lay down into the future tense and it fit like a verb. The bears came home the way weather comes home. They stood around the small bed in their three sizes, and the smallest one said, without heat: you are early. We were not expecting you until yesterday. She has lived there since tomorrow. The porridge, on Tuesdays, is just right, and nobody remarks on it.',)
