# Three Little Pigs - Slipstream

**Logline:** The pigs keep waking in each other's houses. The wolf may be a rumor of weather. Nothing resolves; everything tilts.

## Script

On Monday the first pig woke in the straw house, which was normal, except that he had gone to sleep in the brick one. The town agreed there was a wolf the way a town agrees there was a decade: everyone remembered it differently and no one would say so out loud. The straw house fell, or had always been fallen, or stood somewhere it was inadvisable to visit; the second pig kept its mail in a drawer either way. The stick house was fine except on days ending in wind. The third pig kept a pot boiling because her grandmother had, and her grandmother's grandmother, though none of them could have told you what it was for. Something came down the chimney eventually. It always does. The pot was there, the way the pot is always there, the way some appointments are kept by houses rather than people. On Tuesday they woke in the brick house, all three, unharmed and unsurprised. Outside the window the straw house stood unfallen, with a light on.
