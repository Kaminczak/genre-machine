# Goldilocks and the Three Bears - Superhero Comic

**Logline:** Goldie, teen sidekick, infiltrates the Ursine Bears' safehouse — three thrones, three power-tonics, three regen-beds. The Bears return mid-monologue.

## Script

("PORKHAVEN HEIGHTS, SAFEHOUSE THETA. The Ursine Bears — strongest crime family in three boroughs — keep a brownstone nobody breaches. ENTER GOLDIE, teen sidekick, benched by her mentor for being, quote, too impulsive, which she is about to disprove by impulsively breaching it. THE KITCHEN: three power-tonics on the counter. The first BURNS HER THROAT LIKE JUSTICE — that one is Papa Ursine's, mixed for a man who bench-presses sedans. The second is FLAT — Mama Ursine gave up carbonation in the nineties. The third is JUST RIGHT, cherry, and she drinks it in one panel. THE LOUNGE: three thrones. She breaks the small one just by SITTING WRONG. THE BUNKER: three regen-beds, and she is out cold in the small one before the caption box can warn her. THE BEARS RETURN — mid-monologue, because Papa Ursine narrates his own entrances. SOMEONE HAS CONSUMED MY TONIC. SOMEONE HAS COMPROMISED MY THRONE. The smallest Bear just stares at his occupied regen-bed and says the line that launched a thousand team-ups: 'Dad. She got past all three tiers. We should HIRE her.' NEXT ISSUE: GOLDIE OF THE URSINE SYNDICATE.",)
