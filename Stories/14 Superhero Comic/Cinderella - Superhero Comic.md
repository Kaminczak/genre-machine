# Cinderella - Superhero Comic

**Logline:** Plain-clothes Ella, abused ward, gets powers and a gown from a hero-mentor. The Glass Slipper signal-device falls at the gala. Prince Charming is a secret hero.

## Script

("PORKHAVEN'S GILDED QUARTER, GALA NIGHT. Ella Ashford, ward of the wealthy and terrible Tremaine estate, scrubs the marble her stepfamily struts on — by day. BY NIGHT SHE RUNS THE SOUP LINE IN THE FLATS, because this city eats its poor and somebody has to cook back. ENTER: THE GODMOTHER. Not magic — RETIRED. The Quarter's first vigilante, gray now, cane now, watching the Ashford kid carry a district on her shoulders and seeing, as she says in the iconic panel, THE ONLY HONEST CAPE IN THIS CITY, AND SHE DOESN'T EVEN HAVE ONE YET. The gala is a trap, of course — the Tremaine fortune is mob money, the ball is where the city's masks and monsters shake hands. So the Godmother suits her up: gown with ballistic weave, pumpkin-orange grapnel coupe idling in the alley, and the glass slippers — the old vigilante's own gimmick, resonant-frequency soles, walk on any wall, shatter-proof until, and this is the design flaw she never fixed, MIDNIGHT, when the batteries die. Ella dances with the DA's son, plants a wire on the Tremaine ledger safe, and unmasks half the rogues' gallery by their handshakes. AT 11:59 THE LEDGER ALARM TRIPS. She exits via chandelier. One slipper, batteries dead, stays embedded in the ballroom wall, three meters up. The DA's son pries it out himself. He does not try it on feet. He tries it on ROOFTOPS — walks the city's ledges night after night holding a dead glass shoe, until he finds the soup line in the Flats and the girl who walks on walls. NEXT ISSUE: THE SLIPPER AND THE CITY.",)
