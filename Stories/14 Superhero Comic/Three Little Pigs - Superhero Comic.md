# Three Little Pigs - Superhero Comic

**Logline:** Three pig brothers in color-coded power armor face Wolf, a supervillain who huffs and puffs with sonic lungs.

## Script

THREE BROTHERS. THREE SUITS. ONE VERY BAD DAY FOR THE CITY OF PORKHAVEN. In gold armor, the rookie, HAM-STRIKE, plating rated for parade duty and press photos. In silver, the tactician, IRONHOOF, plating rated for street-level threats. In crimson, the veteran, BULWARK, plating rated for things that have not been invented yet. And rising over the harbor: WOLF, sonic lungs surgically installed, one atmosphere of pure grievance in every breath. His first blast folded gold plating like a parking ticket. His second cracked silver down the sternum. His third hit crimson and rebounded straight back into his own chest, because BULWARK had spent six years designing exactly that. Wolf went for the roof vent, and the roof vent went for Wolf. As the veteran said, hauling both brothers out of the rubble: the strongest armor is the one you built before you knew what was coming.
