# Little Red Riding Hood - Superhero Comic

**Logline:** Red Hood, teen vigilante in scarlet, swings through Wolfsbane City. Big Bad Wolf has taken hostages — gran included.

## Script

WOLFSBANE CITY, 8:47 PM. The Big Bad Wolf has Grandmother Municipal Care Home wrapped in sonic barriers, and his demands are ridiculous: the city's entire supply of respect, delivered by midnight. ENTER RED HOOD. Cape: crimson. Basket: tactical. Patience: expired. She swings the theater district in four moves, drops through the care home's skylight, and lands in a room of hostage grandmothers who all start offering her food at once. THE WOLF TURNS. What big plans you had, Red Hood says, cracking her knuckles. The fight takes two pages and most of the cafeteria. He huffs; she vaults. He puffs; the sprinklers, which she rerouted on page one, answer with pudding from the kitchen line. When the smoke clears, the wolf is zip-tied to a mobility scooter and the grandmothers have adopted him on the spot, which everyone agrees is the harsher sentence. HER grandmother waits by the window. You are late, dear. PATROL RAN LONG, GRAN. There is always cake after.
